## Britaniya va amerikacha nashr

Guess What! paketi **amerikacha** nashrga mos (*color, favorite, mom, soccer*; *Do we have …?*). Round-Up 3 va Destination A1 esa **britaniya** kitoblari: ularda *colour, favourite, football* va Round-Up'ning 2-mini-unitida *have got* o'rgatiladi. Ikkala shakl ham to'g'ri; GW 3-unit bilan RU 2-mini-unitni birga o'tsangiz, o'quvchilarga *have got* (britaniya) va *have* (amerikacha) bir ma'noli ekanini ayting, lekin sinfda bittasiga yopishing.

## Takrorlash va nazorat uchun

- Round-Up **Progress test A** (1–4-mini-unitlar) → GW 1-chorak oxirida; **Progress test B** (5–8) → GW 3-chorak oxirida.
- Destination **Progress test A** (1–5) → GW 2-chorak oxirida; **Progress test B** (6–9) → GW 4-chorak oldidan.
- Chorak testlari faqat Guess What! mazmuniga asoslangan (40 ball): [guess-what/tests/](../guess-what/tests/README.md).
