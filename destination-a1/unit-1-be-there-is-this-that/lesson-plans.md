*3 lessons × 45 minutes · Destination A1 · Grade 3*

_Adapted for Grade 3 (age 8–9, CEFR A1): the topic follows a unit of Destination A1 (grammar and vocabulary practice); all exercises, pictures and texts are new._

### Lesson 1: am, is, are

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | to be with all pronouns; negatives and questions |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • use am / is / are correctly with I, you, he, she, it, we, they<br>• ask and answer yes / no questions with be |
| **Key language** | • I am · she is · they are<br>• I'm not · he isn't · Are you …? Is he …? |
| **Materials** | • Pronoun cards<br>• Grammar card<br>• Worksheet A exercise B; Worksheet B exercises A, C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Greet pupils and introduce yourself: 'I am Miss … I am a teacher.' Write 'I am' on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Who am I?** Hold a picture (a teacher, a doctor); pupils ask 'Are you a teacher?' (yes / no). | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Build the table on the board: I am, you are, he / she / it is, we / you / they are. Add contractions, then negatives and questions. Use classroom examples ('The door is brown. The windows are big.'). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercise B (circle) and Worksheet B exercise A; fast finishers do exercise E (true / false). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Ask and answer**: pupils stand in two lines and ask 'Are you nine?' 'Is your name Bobur?' — short answers only. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says a pronoun, pupils say the form: 'he' → 'is'. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write six sentences about your family with am / is / are.

**Check learning:** Listen for am / is / are in the pairs.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - O'zbek tilida 'bo'lmoq' bog'lovchisi gapda ko'rinmaydi ('Men o'quvchiman'). Inglizchada **am/is/are** shart.
> - Qisqartma: I'm, he's, they're — og'zaki nutqda ko'p ishlatiladi.
> - Support: pronoun–verb matching cards. Extension: add Where / What questions.
>

### Lesson 2: There is, there are

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | there is / there are; there isn't / aren't; Is there …? Are there …? |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • describe a room: There is a board. There are two windows.<br>• ask and answer: Is there a clock? Are there any books? |
| **Key language** | • There is a board. · There are two windows.<br>• There isn't a clock. · Are there any books? — No, there aren't. |
| **Materials** | • A picture of a classroom<br>• Flashcards of classroom things<br>• Worksheet A exercises A, C–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What is in our classroom?' and collect words on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Count and say**: point at things in the room: 'There is one door. There are two windows.' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write the two patterns (there is + one, there are + many). Show negatives and questions. Use a picture of a classroom and change details to practise. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label), C (fill in), D (word search), E (complete words). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Classroom survey**: pairs draw their ideal classroom and describe it: 'There are three computers.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says a number and a noun; pupils make the sentence. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Describe your bedroom with there is / there are (Worksheet B exercise F).

**Check learning:** Check is / are agreement with singular and plural nouns.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'There is + bitta', 'there are + ko'p' — sonlarni doskada raqam bilan ko'rsating: 1 → is, 2+ → are.
> - 'Is there …?' savolida 'any' faqat ko'plik uchun: Are there any books?
> - Support: use pictures with numbers. Extension: add prepositions (on the wall).
>

### Lesson 3: This, that, these, those

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | it's; this / these (near) and that / those (far) |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • use this / these for near things and that / those for far things<br>• say and write: These are my pens. That is the door. |
| **Key language** | • This is a book. · These are books. · That is a door. · Those are doors.<br>• It's a clock. They're clocks. |
| **Materials** | • Objects near and far<br>• Worksheet B exercises B–D |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Hold a book: 'This is a book.' Point to the board: 'That is the board.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Near or far?** Pupils stretch their arm for 'that / those' and keep it close for 'this / these'. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show the 2 × 2 table (near / far, one / many). Practise with real objects at different distances. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises B (circle), C (unscramble) and D (reading). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Classroom shop**: customers point and ask for 'this pen' or 'those books'; the shopkeeper answers 'Here you are.' | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher points; pupils say 'This / That / These / Those …'. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Take four photos or draw four pictures (near / far, one / many) and label them.

**Check learning:** Observe the correct choice in the shop role-play.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Masofani imo-ishora bilan bog'lang: yaqin — qo'l bilan ushlab, uzoq — qo'lni cho'zib ko'rsating.
> - 'these / those' — ko'plik: -s qo'shimchasi ikkalasida ham kerak (books, doors).
> - Support: gestures and real objects. Extension: add adjectives (that big door).
>
