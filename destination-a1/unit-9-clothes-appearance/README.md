## Unit at a glance

- **In the book:** Destination A1, Units 27 and 33 — Vocabulary: Character and appearance; Clothes and fashion
- **Vocabulary:** T-shirt, jeans, dress, cap, shoes, socks, coat, scarf, gloves, hat
- **Grammar:** He is wearing a blue T-shirt. · I'm wearing a coat.
- **Grammar:** She has got long hair. · He has got brown eyes. · They haven't got hats.
- **Grammar:** He is tall. · She is young. · Who is it?

## Lessons

1. Clothes — *Vocabulary — ten clothes words; He is wearing …*
2. What does he look like? — *Appearance: is / has got + tall, short, long hair, brown eyes, young, old*
3. Guess who? — *Describe people and clothes in a guessing game and in writing*
