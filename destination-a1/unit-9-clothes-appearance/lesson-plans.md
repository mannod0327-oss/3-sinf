### Lesson 1: Clothes

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — ten clothes words; He is wearing … |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name ten clothes<br>• say what someone is wearing: He is wearing a blue cap. |
| **Key language** | • T-shirt, jeans, dress, cap, shoes, socks, coat, scarf, gloves, hat<br>• He / She is wearing … |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards (games/flashcards.pdf)<br>• Word card (grammar-card.pdf)<br>• Worksheet A exercises A, B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What are you wearing today?' and write answers on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Who is wearing…?** 'Stand up if you are wearing something blue.' Pupils respond with 'I'm wearing blue socks.' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present the flashcards. Point out plural words (jeans, socks, shoes, gloves). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label), B (match clothes and where we wear them), C (complete) and D (word search). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Dress the doll**: pairs dress a paper figure and describe it: 'It is wearing a red hat.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says a clothes word; pupils say the colour and put a counter on the matching picture. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the words; draw yourself in your favourite clothes (Worksheet A exercise F).

**Check learning:** Point at cards for 5 pupils; note gaps.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'jeans', 'socks', 'shoes', 'gloves' — doim ko'plik: 'These jeans are …' (this jeans emas).
> - 'wear' (kiymoq, kiyib yurmoq) — 'put on' (kiyib olmoq) bilan aralashtirmang.
> - Support: pictures only. Extension: add colours to each item.
>

### Lesson 2: What does he look like?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Appearance: is / has got + tall, short, long hair, brown eyes, young, old |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • describe a person: He is tall. He has got short hair.<br>• use is / has got correctly |
| **Key language** | • He is tall. · She is young. · He has got brown eyes. · She hasn't got short hair. |
| **Materials** | • Pictures of people<br>• Worksheet A exercise E; Worksheet B exercises A–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show a picture: 'Who is this? What does he look like?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Describe and draw**: you describe a face; pupils draw it. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show the two patterns on the board: **is** + adjective; **has got** + hair / eyes. Colour them differently. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises A (circle), B (fill in) and C (unscramble). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **My partner**: pupils describe their partner in three sentences and the partner says 'right' or 'wrong'. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Quick-fire: teacher says a description; pupils say whether it fits a given picture. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Describe a family member in four sentences.

**Check learning:** Check is vs has got.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'He has got long hair' — 'is' bilan emas! 'He is long hair' — keng tarqalgan xato.
> - 'short' ikki ma'noli: past bo'yli (short) va kalta (short hair).
> - Support: sentence frames. Extension: add colours of hair and eyes.
>

### Lesson 3: Guess who?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Describe people and clothes in a guessing game and in writing |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • describe a person with clothes and appearance<br>• write 5 sentences about a friend |
| **Key language** | • He is tall. He has got short hair. He is wearing a red cap and jeans. Who is he? |
| **Materials** | • A picture of 6 people<br>• Worksheet B exercises D–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show the picture of 6 people: 'I'm thinking of one person.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Yes / no**: pupils ask 'Is it a boy? Has he got short hair?' to guess. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Read the model text (Worksheet B exercise D) and underline is / has got / is wearing. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises D (reading) and E (writing). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Guess who?** In pairs: one pupil describes a classmate; the other guesses. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Volunteers describe a classmate and the class guesses. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Describe a friend or family member and draw them.

**Check learning:** Mark Worksheet B exercise E for is / has got / is wearing.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - O'yin paytida bolalar ismini aytmasin — faqat tasvir. Hurmat qoidasi: tashqi ko'rinish haqida xushmuomala gapiring.
> - Support: gapped model text. Extension: add 'but' (He is tall but she is short).
>
