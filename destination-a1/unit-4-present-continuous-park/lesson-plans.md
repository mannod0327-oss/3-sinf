# Unit 4 · Present continuous: at the park — Lesson plans

*3 lessons × 45 minutes · Destination A1 · Grade 3*

_Adapted for Grade 3 (age 8–9, CEFR A1): the topic follows a unit of Destination A1 (grammar and vocabulary practice); all exercises, pictures and texts are new._

### Unit at a glance

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **In the book** | Destination A1, Unit 5 — Present continuous |
| **Topic** | What are people doing now? |
| **Vocabulary** | wash my hands, listen to music, talk, eat lunch, drink juice, ride a bike, fly a kite, take photos, play chess, climb a tree |
| **Grammar** | • am / is / are + verb-ing: She is riding a bike.<br>• negative: He isn't eating. · They aren't talking.<br>• questions: What are you doing? · Is he flying a kite? — Yes, he is. |

### Lesson 1: At the park

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — ten actions people do in the park |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name ten actions<br>• say what a person is doing in a picture |
| **Key language** | • wash my hands, listen to music, talk, eat lunch, drink juice, ride a bike, fly a kite, take photos, play chess, climb a tree |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards (games/flashcards.pdf)<br>• A big picture of a park with many people<br>• Worksheet A exercises A–B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show a picture of a park: 'Where is this? What can you see?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Mime and guess**: pupils mime actions; the class says 'You are riding a bike!' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present the ten flashcards with actions. Choral repetition. Stick cards on the park picture. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label), B (match with Uzbek) and C (complete the words). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Spot the person**: you describe a person in the park picture ('He is flying a kite.'); pupils point. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher mimes; pupils say the action. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the ten phrases and draw a park scene (Worksheet A exercise F).

**Check learning:** Point at cards for 5 pupils; note gaps.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'take photos' va 'take a photo' — ikkalasi ham to'g'ri; 'make photos' xato.
> - 'wash my hands', 'fly a kite' — butun ibora sifatida yodlating.
> - Support: pictures only. Extension: add 'feed ducks', 'play football'.
>

### Lesson 2: She is riding a bike

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Present continuous: statements and negatives; -ing spelling |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say and write what people are doing and are not doing<br>• spell -ing forms (riding, taking, running) |
| **Key language** | • She is riding a bike. · They aren't talking.<br>• ride → riding · take → taking · sit → sitting |
| **Materials** | • Park picture<br>• Worksheet A exercises C–E; Worksheet B exercise A |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What are you doing now?' — 'I'm sitting. I'm listening.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Freeze!** Pupils mime an action; you freeze them: 'What is he doing?' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write am / is / are + -ing on the board and the three spelling rules with examples. Use the park picture: 'The girl is flying a kite. The boys aren't eating.' | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises C (complete), D (word search) and E (-ing forms); Worksheet B exercise A (circle). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Describe the picture**: pairs write five sentences about people in the park picture. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Pairs read one sentence aloud; the class says true or false. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write six sentences about the people in a picture.

**Check learning:** Check am / is / are and -ing spelling.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - O'zbekcha '-yapti' = am/is/are + -ing. 'She riding' xatosi: 'is' ni qo'shing.
> - Imlo: ride → riding (e tushadi), run → running (undosh ikkilanadi).
> - Support: spelling cards. Extension: add negatives for every sentence.
>

### Lesson 3: What are they doing?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Present continuous: questions and short answers |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • ask and answer: What is he doing? Is she riding a bike? — Yes, she is.<br>• describe a picture in 5 sentences |
| **Key language** | • What are you doing? · Is he flying a kite? — Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.<br>• Are they talking? — Yes, they are. |
| **Materials** | • Picture cards<br>• Worksheet B exercises B–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Mime an action and ask 'What am I doing?' Elicit full sentences. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Yes / no guess**: a pupil chooses a hidden action card; the class asks 'Are you riding a bike?' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write the question patterns on the board: What + am / is / are + subject + -ing? and Is / Are + subject + -ing? with short answers. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises B (fill in), C (unscramble) and D (reading). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Park detectives**: pairs have two park pictures with five differences; they ask 'Is the boy eating?' to find them. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Report: 'In my picture the girl is flying a kite, but in yours she is riding a bike.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write five questions about a picture and their answers.

**Check learning:** Listen for inversion in questions (Is he …? not He is …?).

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Savol: 'Is he flying?' (Is oldinda). O'zbekchada '-mi' yuklamasi — inglizchada so'z tartibi o'zgaradi.
> - Qisqa javob: Yes, he **is**. (not 'Yes, he's').
>
