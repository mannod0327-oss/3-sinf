## Unit at a glance

- **In the book:** Destination A1, Unit 5 — Present continuous
- **Vocabulary:** wash my hands, listen to music, talk, eat lunch, drink juice, ride a bike, fly a kite, take photos, play chess, climb a tree
- **Grammar:** am / is / are + verb-ing: She is riding a bike.
- **Grammar:** negative: He isn't eating. · They aren't talking.
- **Grammar:** questions: What are you doing? · Is he flying a kite? — Yes, he is.

## Lessons

1. At the park — *Vocabulary — ten actions people do in the park*
2. She is riding a bike — *Present continuous: statements and negatives; -ing spelling*
3. What are they doing? — *Present continuous: questions and short answers*
