*3 lessons × 45 minutes · Destination A1 · Grade 3*

_Adapted for Grade 3 (age 8–9, CEFR A1): the topic follows a unit of Destination A1 (grammar and vocabulary practice); all exercises, pictures and texts are new._

### Unit at a glance

|  |  |
|---|---|

### Lesson 1: What's the weather like?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — weather words; It's sunny / rainy / windy / cloudy / snowy |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name seven weather words<br>• ask and answer: What's the weather like? It's sunny. |
| **Key language** | • sunny, rainy, windy, cloudy, snowy, hot, cold, stormy<br>• What's the weather like? — It's … |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards (games/flashcards.pdf)<br>• Word card (grammar-card.pdf)<br>• Worksheet A exercises A, B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Look out of the window: 'What's the weather like today?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Weather mime**: pupils mime weather (shiver for cold, fan for hot, blow for windy); the class guesses. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present the flashcards. Teach 'It's + adjective'. Choral repetition with actions. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label), C (complete) and D (word search). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Weather report**: pairs make a TV weather report for four cities with weather pictures. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher shows a card; pupils say 'It's windy.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the weather words; draw today's weather (Worksheet A exercise F).

**Check learning:** Point at cards for 5 pupils; note gaps.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'It's sunny' — 'It' ob-havoda majburiy: 'Is sunny' xato. O'zbekchada 'Havo ochiq' — egasi yo'q.
> - 'hot' (havo issiq) va 'warm' (iliq) farqi — 'cold' va 'cool' bilan solishtiring.
> - Support: pictures only. Extension: add 'foggy', 'stormy'.
>

### Lesson 2: Four seasons

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Seasons and their weather; Navro'z |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name the four seasons and say the weather: In summer it's hot.<br>• match seasons with months |
| **Key language** | • spring, summer, autumn, winter<br>• In spring it's warm and rainy. · In winter it's cold and snowy. |
| **Materials** | • Season posters<br>• Worksheet A exercise B; Worksheet B exercises A–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Which season is it now? What's your favourite season?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Season corners**: four corners of the room are the four seasons; pupils run to the season you call. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show a year circle with months and seasons. Link Navro'z (21 March) with spring. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercise B (match seasons and weather); Worksheet B exercises A (circle), B (fill in) and C (unscramble). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Season talk**: pairs ask 'What's the weather like in winter?' and answer for Uzbekistan. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher names a month; pupils say the season. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write four sentences about the four seasons where you live.

**Check learning:** Check In + season and It's + adjective.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - O'zbekistonda 4 fasl aniq: bahor (Navro'z), yoz (juda issiq), kuz, qish. Mahalliy tajribadan foydalaning.
> - 'autumn' /ˈɔːtəm/ — 'n' aytilmaydi.
> - Support: month cards. Extension: add 'because' reasons.
>

### Lesson 3: My favourite season

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Opinions and clothes: I like … because …; We wear … in … |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say their favourite season and why<br>• link weather and clothes: We wear coats in winter. |
| **Key language** | • I like summer because it's hot. · We wear coats in winter. · I don't like winter because it's cold. |
| **Materials** | • Clothes flashcards (coat, scarf, gloves, hat, cap)<br>• Worksheet B exercises D–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show a coat and a hat: 'When do we wear these?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **What do we wear?** Call a weather word; pupils name clothes. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Model: 'I like autumn because it's windy and I fly a kite.' Write the frame on the board. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises D (reading) and E (writing). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Season poster**: groups draw one season with weather, clothes and activities and present it. | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Gallery walk: pupils vote for their favourite poster. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Finish your text and add a picture.

**Check learning:** Poster rubric: weather (1), clothes (1), sentences (1).

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Sabab bog'lovchisi: 'because'. Gapni 'because' dan boshlamang: 'I like summer because it's hot.'
> - Support: gapped model text. Extension: compare two seasons.
>
