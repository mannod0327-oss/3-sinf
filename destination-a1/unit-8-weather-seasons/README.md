## Unit at a glance

- **In the book:** Destination A1, Unit 30 — Vocabulary: Weather and seasons, nature and ecology
- **Vocabulary:** sunny, rainy, windy, cloudy, snowy, hot, cold, spring, summer, autumn, winter
- **Grammar:** What's the weather like? — It's sunny / It's raining.
- **Grammar:** In summer it's hot. · In winter it's cold and snowy.
- **Grammar:** I like summer because it's hot. · We wear coats in winter.

## Lessons

1. What's the weather like? — *Vocabulary — weather words; It's sunny / rainy / windy / cloudy / snowy*
2. Four seasons — *Seasons and their weather; Navro'z*
