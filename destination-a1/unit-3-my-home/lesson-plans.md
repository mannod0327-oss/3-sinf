# Unit 3 · My home — Lesson plans

*3 lessons × 45 minutes · Destination A1 · Grade 3*

_Adapted for Grade 3 (age 8–9, CEFR A1): the topic follows a unit of Destination A1 (grammar and vocabulary practice); all exercises, pictures and texts are new._

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **In the book** | Destination A1, Unit 3 — Vocabulary: My home |
| **Topic** | Rooms and furniture; where things are |
| **Vocabulary** | living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, garden, door, window, sofa, lamp, chair |

### Lesson 1: Rooms in my home

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — rooms and what we do in them |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name five rooms and the garden<br>• say what we do in each room: I sleep in the bedroom. |
| **Key language** | • living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, garden<br>• I sleep / cook / wash / watch TV in the … |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards (games/flashcards.pdf)<br>• Word card (grammar-card.pdf)<br>• Worksheet A exercises A, B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Do you live in a flat or a house?' and write the answers on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Floor plan**: draw a simple plan of a flat on the board and name the rooms with the class. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present the room flashcards, one at a time, and attach an action: bedroom = sleep, kitchen = cook, bathroom = wash. Choral repetition. Stick the cards on the plan. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label), B (match sentences to rooms) and C (complete the words). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Where am I?** One pupil mimes (sleeping, cooking); the class asks 'Are you in the bedroom?' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says an action; pupils say the room. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Draw a plan of your home and label four rooms (Worksheet A exercise F).

**Check learning:** Point at cards for 5 pupils; note gaps.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'in the kitchen' (artikl bilan), lekin 'at home' (artiklsiz) — alohida eslatib qo'ying.
> - 'living room' — mehmonxona; ko'p o'zbek oilalarida 'zal' deyiladi. Ikkalasi ham bir xil xona.
> - Support: use the picture card. Extension: add 'balcony' and 'hall'.
>

### Lesson 2: Furniture and where it is

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Furniture words and prepositions: in, on, next to, under, behind |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name furniture (sofa, lamp, chair, window, door)<br>• say where it is: The lamp is on the table. |
| **Key language** | • The lamp is on the table. · The chair is next to the window.<br>• in · on · under · next to · behind |
| **Materials** | • Furniture flashcards<br>• Preposition pictures from the Round-Up pack (or a ball and a box)<br>• Worksheet A D–E; B exercises A–B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Hold a lamp or its picture: 'Where is the lamp? It's on the table.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Where's the ball?** Hide a ball and ask 'Is it under the chair?' until pupils find it. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present furniture words with the flashcards. Revise prepositions with real objects. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises D (word search) and E (odd one out); Worksheet B exercises A (circle) and B (fill in). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Describe and draw**: pairs describe a room ('There is a sofa next to the window.') while the partner draws. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Check the drawings against the descriptions. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write four sentences about furniture in your bedroom.

**Check learning:** Check correct prepositions in the describe-and-draw task.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'in / on / under' — rasm yoki haqiqiy buyum bilan; o'zbekcha '-da, ustida, tagida'.
> - 'There is / are' oldingi bo'limda — takrorlab o'ting.
> - Support: preposition pictures. Extension: add 'between'.
>

### Lesson 3: My dream home

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Describe a home: There is / are, has got, adjectives |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • describe their home or dream home in 4–5 sentences<br>• ask and answer about each other's homes |
| **Key language** | • There is a big garden. · There are two bedrooms. · My room isn't big.<br>• Is there a …? Yes, there is. |
| **Materials** | • Paper and crayons<br>• Worksheet B exercises C–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show a picture of a big house: 'Is this your home? No, it's my dream home.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Yes or no?** Pupils ask about a hidden picture of a home: 'Is there a garden?' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Model a description on the board using the frames from Worksheet B exercise E, then read the sample text (Worksheet B exercise D). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises C (unscramble), D (reading) and E (writing). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Home interview**: pairs ask 'How many rooms are there? Is there a garden?' and draw each other's home. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Gallery walk: pupils read two descriptions and tick one sentence they like. Homework. | Ss-Ss |

**Homework:** Finish your description and add a drawing.

**Check learning:** Mark Worksheet B exercise E for there is / are and room vocabulary.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Hikoya yozishda ketma-ketlik: avval xonalar, keyin mebel, so'ng bog'.
> - Support: gapped model text. Extension: add adjectives (big, small, new, old, beautiful).
>
