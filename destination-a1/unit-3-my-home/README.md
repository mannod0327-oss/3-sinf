## Unit at a glance

- **In the book:** Destination A1, Unit 3 — Vocabulary: My home
- **Vocabulary:** living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, garden, door, window, sofa, lamp, chair
- **Grammar:** There is / there are + a room or a thing; Is there …? Are there …?
- **Grammar:** Where is the lamp? It's on / in / next to / under the …
- **Grammar:** I sleep in the bedroom. We cook in the kitchen.

## Lessons

1. Rooms in my home — *Vocabulary — rooms and what we do in them*
2. Furniture and where it is — *Furniture words and prepositions: in, on, next to, under, behind*
3. My dream home — *Describe a home: There is / are, has got, adjectives*
