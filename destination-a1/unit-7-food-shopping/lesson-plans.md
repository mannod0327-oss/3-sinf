### Lesson 1: Food words

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — ten food and drink words; countable and uncountable |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name ten foods and drinks<br>• say if a noun is countable (eggs) or uncountable (milk) |
| **Key language** | • bread, milk, cheese, rice, eggs, apples, tomatoes, potatoes, water, juice<br>• some · any |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards (games/flashcards.pdf)<br>• Word card (grammar-card.pdf)<br>• Worksheet A exercises A, E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What do you have for breakfast?' and write answers on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Feel and guess**: pupils touch real food or toys in a bag and guess the word. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present the flashcards. Sort them into two columns: things we can count (eggs, apples) and things we can't (milk, rice). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label), B (match quantities), C (complete the words) and E (odd one out). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **My shopping basket**: pupils draw a basket with five items and say 'I've got some bread and two apples.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says a word; pupils say 'countable' or 'uncountable'. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the words; draw your breakfast (Worksheet A exercise F).

**Check learning:** Check pupils' sorting into countable and uncountable.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'non', 'sut', 'guruch', 'suv' — sanalmaydigan; 'two breads' xato. 'a loaf of bread' deb ayting.
> - Ko'plik: tomato → tomatoes, potato → potatoes (-es). Eslatib o'ting.
> - Support: pictures only. Extension: add 'honey', 'sugar'.
>

### Lesson 2: How much? A kilo of…

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Quantities and prices: a kilo of, a bottle of, a packet of, a loaf of; How much is it? |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • use a kilo of, a bottle of, a packet of, a loaf of, a box of<br>• ask and answer about prices in sum |
| **Key language** | • a kilo of apples · a bottle of milk · a packet of rice · a loaf of bread · a box of eggs<br>• How much is it? — It's 4,000 sum. |
| **Materials** | • Price tags (in sum) for pictures or real items<br>• Worksheet A exercise B; Worksheet B exercises A–B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Hold up a price tag: 'How much is the bread? It's 4,000 sum.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Price guess**: pupils guess the price of an item; closest wins. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show each quantity phrase with a picture. Write 'How much is it?' and 'How much are they?' with answers. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercise B (match) and Worksheet B exercises A (circle) and B (complete the dialogue). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Price chain**: pupils pass a tag around: 'How much are the apples?' — 'They're 10,000 sum.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says a price; pupils say what it could be. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Make a price list of five items at home or in a shop.

**Check learning:** Check it's / they're with prices.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'How much is the bread?' (birlik) / 'How much are the apples?' (ko'plik) — is / are farqiga e'tibor bering.
> - Raqam: 4,000 = four thousand. Katta sonlarni sekin o'qing.
> - Support: price tags with numbers written in words. Extension: add change (change — qaytim).
>

### Lesson 3: At the shop

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Role-play: I'd like … Can I have …? How much is it? |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • buy and sell food in a role-play<br>• write a short shop dialogue |
| **Key language** | • Can I help you? — I'd like a loaf of bread, please. — Here you are. — How much is it? |
| **Materials** | • Play money (sum)<br>• Food picture cards<br>• Worksheet B exercises C–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Set up a shop corner with cards and play money. 'Welcome to my shop!' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Shopkeeper's memory**: pupils name items on the table; you cover one — which is missing? | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Model the dialogue with a volunteer. Write the key phrases on the board. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises C (unscramble), D (reading) and E (writing a dialogue). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Shop role-play**: pairs swap roles (shopkeeper / customer) and buy three things each. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Volunteers perform for the class; the class says what they bought. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write a four-line shop dialogue.

**Check learning:** Observe I'd like / Can I have / How much in the role-play.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Muloyim iboralar: **please**, **thank you**, **here you are**. Ularni doim talab qiling.
> - 'I'd like' = 'I would like' (istayman) — qisqartma sifatida o'rgating.
> - Support: dialogue frame. Extension: add 'Anything else?'
>
