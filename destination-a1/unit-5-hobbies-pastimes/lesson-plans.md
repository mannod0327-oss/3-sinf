### Lesson 1: My free time

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — ten hobbies; play / go collocations |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name ten hobbies<br>• use play, go and other verbs with the right hobby |
| **Key language** | • play chess / football / computer games · go cycling / swimming / fishing · read books · draw pictures · dance · sing |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards (games/flashcards.pdf)<br>• Word card (grammar-card.pdf)<br>• Worksheet A exercises A, B, E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What do you do in your free time?' and write answers in two columns (play / go). | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Charades**: pupils mime hobbies; the class guesses 'You play chess!' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present the ten flashcards with actions. Group them by verb: **play** (chess, football, computer games), **go** (cycling, swimming, fishing), others. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label), B (match with Uzbek) and C (play / go / – circle). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Hobby bingo**: pupils write five hobbies on a grid; the teacher mimes, pupils cross out. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says the hobby; pupils say the full phrase. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the ten phrases and draw your favourite hobby (Worksheet A exercise F).

**Check learning:** Check that pupils use play / go correctly.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'play chess' (o'ynamoq) lekin 'go swimming' (-ga bormoq): fe'l hobbyga bog'liq — jadval qiling.
> - 'draw pictures', 'read books' — fe'l o'zi yetarli (play / go yo'q).
> - Support: pictures only. Extension: add 'play the guitar', 'do karate'.
>

### Lesson 2: I like dancing

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | like / love / enjoy / hate + -ing |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say what they like and don't like doing: I like dancing. I don't like fishing.<br>• ask and answer: Do you like swimming? — Yes, I do. |
| **Key language** | • I like / love / enjoy / hate + -ing<br>• Do you like …ing? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't. |
| **Materials** | • Hobby flashcards<br>• Emotion cards (like, love, enjoy, hate)<br>• Worksheet B exercises A–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Do you like swimming?' — thumbs up or down. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Like line**: pupils stand on a line from 'hate' to 'love' for each hobby you call. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write the pattern: I like / love / enjoy / hate + verb-ing. Show -ing endings (dance → dancing, swim → swimming). Show questions and short answers. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises A (circle), B (fill in) and C (unscramble). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Class survey**: pupils ask three classmates 'What do you like doing?' and record answers in a table. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Report: 'Aziz likes playing chess. Malika loves dancing.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write five sentences with like / love / enjoy / hate + -ing.

**Check learning:** Check -ing after like and he / she + s in the reports.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'like' dan keyin fe'l + -ing: 'I like dance' — xato; 'I like dancing'.
> - Report'da: He like**s** …: -s unutilmasin.
> - Support: sentence frames. Extension: add reasons with 'because'.
>

### Lesson 3: Hobby poster

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Describe my hobbies in writing and speaking |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • write 4–5 sentences about their hobbies<br>• present a hobby poster to the class |
| **Key language** | • I love …ing. · I like …ing. · I don't like …ing. · I go … on Saturdays. |
| **Materials** | • Paper, crayons<br>• Worksheet B exercises D–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show an example poster ('My hobbies') with drawings and words. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Guess my hobby**: you give three clues; pupils guess. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Read the model text (Worksheet B exercise D) and underline like / love / enjoy + -ing. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises D (reading) and E (writing). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Hobby poster**: pupils draw their hobbies and write three sentences, then present them in groups. | S / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Gallery walk: pupils tick a hobby they also like. Homework. | G / T-Ss |

**Homework:** Finish your poster and ask a family member about their hobbies.

**Check learning:** Poster rubric: hobbies (1), sentences (1), neatness (1).

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Taqdimot: har bir bola 2–3 gap aytsin; xatolarni darhol tuzatmang, oxirida umumiy fikr bildiring.
> - Support: gapped text. Extension: compare two friends' hobbies.
>
