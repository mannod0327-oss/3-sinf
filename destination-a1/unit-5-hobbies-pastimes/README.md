## Unit at a glance

- **In the book:** Destination A1, Unit 6 — Vocabulary: Hobbies and pastimes
- **Vocabulary:** play chess, play football, go cycling, go swimming, read books, draw pictures, dance, sing, play computer games, go fishing
- **Grammar:** like / love / enjoy / hate + verb-ing: I like dancing.
- **Grammar:** play + game, go + -ing activity, read / draw / sing: collocations
- **Grammar:** What do you like doing? · Do you like reading? — Yes, I do.

## Lessons

1. My free time — *Vocabulary — ten hobbies; play / go collocations*
