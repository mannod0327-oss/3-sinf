### Lesson 1: School subjects

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — subjects, teacher, pupil, homework |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name seven school subjects and three school words<br>• say what you do in each subject |
| **Key language** | • Maths, English, Science, Art, Music, PE, Geography, teacher, pupil, homework |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards (games/flashcards.pdf)<br>• Word card (grammar-card.pdf)<br>• Worksheet A exercises A–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What subjects do you have at school?' and write the answers on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Mime the lesson**: pupils mime a subject (painting, singing, running); the class says 'Art! Music! PE!' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present the flashcards with an action each (Maths = count on fingers, Art = draw). Choral repetition. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label), B (match), C (complete the words). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **What do you do in …?** Pairs ask 'What do you do in Music?' — 'We sing.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says an action; pupils say the subject. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the words; write your timetable in English (Worksheet A exercise F).

**Check learning:** Point at cards for 5 pupils; note gaps.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Fanlar nomi bosh harf bilan yoziladi (Maths, English, Art). 'PE' = 'physical education'.
> - 'Science' o'zbek maktabida 'tabiiy fan' (3–4-sinf) — o'sha fan.
> - Support: pictures only. Extension: add 'History' and 'IT'.
>

### Lesson 2: Our timetable

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | We have … on …; the teacher gives us … |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • talk about the timetable: We have Maths on Monday.<br>• ask and answer: What do you have on Tuesday? |
| **Key language** | • We have Maths on Monday. · What do you have on Tuesday? — We have Science and Art. |
| **Materials** | • Class timetable<br>• Worksheet B exercises A–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show the class timetable: 'What do we have today?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Timetable bingo**: pupils write five day–subject pairs and cross them out when called. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write 'We have + subject + on + day' and the question with an example from the timetable. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises A (circle), B (fill in) and C (unscramble). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Timetable interview**: pairs compare timetables: 'What do you have on Friday?' — 'We have PE.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher names a day; pupils say the subjects. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write four sentences about your timetable.

**Check learning:** Check have / has and on + day.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'We have Maths' — o'zbekchada 'bizda matematika bor'. 'have' dan keyin 'got' shart emas (Destination'da 'have').
> - Kun oldidagi predlog: on Monday; kun qismi: in the morning.
>

### Lesson 3: I like it because…

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Opinions: favourite subject, easy / difficult / fun / boring, because |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say their favourite subject and give a reason<br>• write 4–5 sentences about school |
| **Key language** | • My favourite subject is Art. · I like Art because it's fun. · Maths is difficult, but I like it. |
| **Materials** | • Opinion cards (easy, difficult, fun, boring)<br>• Worksheet B exercises D–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What's your favourite subject?' and make a class chart. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Opinion line**: pupils stand on a line from 'boring' to 'fun' for each subject. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Model: 'I like English because it's fun.' Write because on the board with the four opinion words. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises D (reading) and E (writing). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Class survey poster**: groups ask classmates and make a chart: 'Art is our favourite subject.' | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Report the survey results. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write why you like one subject and don't like another.

**Check learning:** Mark Worksheet B exercise E for because and opinion words.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'because' = chunki; gap ikkiga bo'linadi: I like Art **because** it's fun (nuqta qo'ymang).
> - 'boring' (zerikarli) va 'bored' (zerikkan) ni aralashtirmang — hozircha faqat 'boring'.
> - Support: opinion cards. Extension: compare two subjects.
>
