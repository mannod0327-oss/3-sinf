### Lesson 1: Jobs

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — ten jobs and what they do |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name ten jobs<br>• say what people do: A pilot flies planes. |
| **Key language** | • teacher, doctor, cook, farmer, pilot, singer, artist, firefighter, police officer, mechanic<br>• A teacher teaches. · A doctor helps people. |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards (games/flashcards.pdf)<br>• Word card (grammar-card.pdf)<br>• Worksheet A exercises A–B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What do your parents do?' and collect jobs in Uzbek and English. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Mime the job**: pupils mime a job; the class guesses in English. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present the ten job flashcards with a typical action for each and a short sentence ('A cook makes food.'). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label) and B (match job and action). Play **What's missing?** | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Who am I?** Pairs: one pupil says 'I fly planes. Who am I?' The partner answers 'You are a pilot.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says an action; pupils say the job. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the jobs; ask two adults about their jobs and write them in English.

**Check learning:** Flash cards at random for 5 pupils; note gaps.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Kasblar oldidan 'a' / 'an' qo'yiladi: a teacher, an artist — ikkalasini mashq qiling.
> - 'police officer' — ikki so'z; 'police' ko'plik sifatida ishlatiladi ('The police are here').
> - Support: match jobs with pictures only. Extension: add 'at work' places (school, hospital).
>

### Lesson 2: He teaches, she cooks

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Present simple: he / she + verb-s; I / you / we / they + verb |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • use the third-person -s / -es / -ies<br>• contrast: Pilots fly. A pilot flies. |
| **Key language** | • A teacher teaches children. · Pilots fly planes.<br>• flies · goes · does · studies |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards<br>• Worksheet A exercises C–E; Worksheet B exercise A |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Say 'A teacher teaches' on the board and underline -es. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Verb race**: two teams write the third-person form of a verb on the board. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write two columns: I / you / we / they (base verb) and he / she / it (+ s, + es, + ies). Sort ten verbs. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises C (complete), D (odd one out) and E (word search); Worksheet B exercise A. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Job riddles**: pupils write two sentences about a job ('He helps sick people. He works in a hospital.') and read them for the class to guess. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Quick-fire: teacher says 'pilot — fly'; pupils say 'A pilot flies planes.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write five sentences about jobs in your family (he / she + verb-s).

**Check learning:** Check for missing -s in the riddles.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Uchinchi shaxs -s — o'zbekchada yo'q. Doskada qizil bilan yozing va har gapdan keyin tekshiring.
> - 'flies', 'studies': y → ies qoidasini jadval bilan ko'rsating.
> - Support: give the -s ending on cards. Extension: add adverbs (always, usually).
>

### Lesson 3: Does a cook fly planes?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Present simple: negatives, questions and short answers |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say what people don't do: A doctor doesn't cook.<br>• ask and answer: Does a pilot fly planes? Yes, he does. |
| **Key language** | • A doctor doesn't cook. · Farmers don't fly planes.<br>• Does a cook make food? — Yes, he does. / Do pilots fly? — Yes, they do. |
| **Materials** | • Job cards<br>• Worksheet B exercises A–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Does a teacher cook food?' — pupils answer 'No, he doesn't.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **True or false?** Read sentences about jobs; pupils stand for true and sit for false. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write the negative and question patterns on the board; underline that the verb has no -s after doesn't / does. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises B (fill in), C (unscramble) and D (reading). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Guess the job**: a pupil chooses a job card; the class asks 'Does he fly planes?' (only yes / no) until they guess. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher reads three sentences; pupils correct the wrong one. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write four questions about jobs and their answers.

**Check learning:** Listen for Do / Does and the bare verb.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'Does he teaches?' — xato. 'Does' dan keyin fe'l asl holda: 'Does he teach?'.
> - Qisqa javob: Yes, he **does**. / No, he **doesn't**.
>
