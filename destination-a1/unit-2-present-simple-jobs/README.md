## Unit at a glance

- **In the book:** Destination A1, Units 2 and 4 — Present simple 1 and 2
- **Vocabulary:** teacher, doctor, cook, farmer, pilot, singer, artist, firefighter, police officer, mechanic
- **Grammar:** he / she / it + verb-s: A teacher teaches children.
- **Grammar:** I / you / we / they + verb: Pilots fly planes.
- **Grammar:** doesn't / don't + verb; Do / Does + subject + verb?

## Lessons

1. Jobs — *Vocabulary — ten jobs and what they do*
2. He teaches, she cooks — *Present simple: he / she + verb-s; I / you / we / they + verb*
3. Does a cook fly planes? — *Present simple: negatives, questions and short answers*
