### Lesson 1: Fruit and vegetables

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — ten fruit and vegetables (plural nouns) |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson students can…) | • name ten fruit and vegetables in the plural<br>• spell plurals: lemons, tomatoes, mangoes, onions |
| **Key language** | • lemons, limes, watermelons, coconuts, grapes, mangoes, pineapples, pears, tomatoes, onions |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards or real fruit<br>• Student's Book Vocabulary page + audio<br>• Worksheet A exercises A–B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Bring a bag with fruit. Ask 'What's in my bag?' and write **At the market** on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Feel and guess**: a student puts a hand in the bag and guesses the fruit by touch; class helps with Uzbek words. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present the ten words with flashcards or real items. Stress the plural: 'One lemon. Two lemons.' Note the spelling: tomato → tomatoes, mango → mangoes. Student's Book: *Listen and point*, *Listen, point and repeat*. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label) and B (match singular and plural). Play **What's missing?** | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Market stall**: pairs build a stall on the desk with cards and sell: 'Two mangoes, please.' — 'Here you are.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher holds up a card; students say the plural word. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the words and draw your favorite fruit stall (Worksheet A exercise F).

**Check learning:** Point at cards for 5 students; note plural errors.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - O'zbek bozorida ('bozor') bu mevalarning ko'pi bor — mahalliy nomlar bilan bog'lang: uzum, olma, nok, tarvuz.
> - Ko'plik: -o bilan tugaydigan so'zlar (tomato, mango, potato) → -es. Bular istisno; jadval qilib yozing.
> - Support: flashcards with pictures only. Extension: students write a color next to each fruit.
>

### Lesson 2: There are lots of grapes

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Grammar 1 — There are lots of / some + plural nouns |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson students can…) | • describe a market stall: There are lots of grapes. There are some tomatoes.<br>• choose lots of (many) or some (a few) |
| **Key language** | • There (are lots of) (grapes). There (are some) (tomatoes). |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards in groups on the board (8 grapes, 3 tomatoes)<br>• Student's Book Grammar page 1<br>• Worksheet B exercise A |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'How many students are there in our class?' and count together. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **How many?** Show a card with a number of dots or fruit for three seconds; students guess 'lots' or 'some'. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Draw a stall on the board: 10 grapes, 3 tomatoes. Say: 'There are lots of grapes. There are some tomatoes.' Write the frame: There are lots of / some + plural noun. Drill with changing pictures. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Student's Book Grammar activities (listen and check). Worksheet B exercise A (circle). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Stall descriptions**: students draw their own stall with different amounts and describe it to a partner, who draws what they hear. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Quick-fire: show a picture; students say 'There are lots of …' or 'some …'. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Workbook grammar page; describe your kitchen: 'There are some …'

**Check learning:** Check are + plural and lots of / some.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'There are' + ko'plik. O'zbekcha 'bor' bir xil, lekin inglizchada **is** (birlik) va **are** (ko'plik) farqlanadi.
> - 'lots of' = 'a lot of' (ko'p); suhbatda juda ko'p ishlatiladi.
> - Support: gesture for lots (arms wide) and some (two fingers). Extension: add colors.
>

### Lesson 3: Are there any pears?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Grammar 2 — There aren't any … / Are there any …? Yes, there are. / No, there aren't. |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson students can…) | • say what is not there: There aren't any limes.<br>• ask and answer: Are there any pears? Yes, there are. / No, there aren't. |
| **Key language** | • There (aren't any) (limes).<br>• Are there any (pears)? — Yes, there are. / No, there aren't. |
| **Materials** | • A stall picture with some items missing<br>• Chant from the audio<br>• Worksheet B exercises B–D |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show an empty basket: 'Are there any apples?' — 'No, there aren't.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Fruit basket**: show a covered picture; students guess with 'Are there any pears?' (you answer yes / no). | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show a stall with only grapes and onions. Model: 'Are there any pears? No, there aren't. There aren't any pears.' Write the pattern and color any. Compare some (positive) and any (negative and questions). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Play the chant / Student's Book Grammar audio. Worksheet B exercises B (read and answer), C (fill in) and D (unscramble). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Spot the difference**: pairs have two stall pictures; they ask 'Are there any …?' to find five differences. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher asks about a picture; students answer with full short answers. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Workbook grammar page; write three sentences about a picture of a shop.

**Check learning:** Check some / any and short answers.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - **some** — darak gap; **any** — inkor va so'roq gap. Bu qoida o'zbek tilida yo'q; rangli qilib yozing.
> - 'aren't' /ɑrnt/ — amerikacha talaffuzda 'r' aytiladi. 'There aren't any' uch so'zni bir ovozda o'qishni mashq qiling.
> - Support: sentence frames. Extension: add 'but there are some …'.
>

### Lesson 4: Do you like smoothies?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Skills: Listening — Do you like smoothies? + writing a recipe |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson students can…) | • listen for fruit and numbers in a smoothie recipe<br>• write a short smoothie recipe |
| **Key language** | • smoothie · milk · ice · blend<br>• There are … / Put … in the glass. |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book Listening page + audio<br>• Worksheet B exercise E<br>• recipe cards |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Do you like smoothies?' and count hands. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Design a smoothie**: students call out fruit; write a class smoothie on the board. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Pre-listening: look at the pictures and name the fruit. Play the audio twice: first for gist, then to check the ingredients. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Check in pairs, then class. Students read the market text (Worksheet B exercise B) to find what there is. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | Writing: students write a short recipe: 'My smoothie: two bananas, some grapes…'. Volunteers read aloud. | S |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Class votes for the best smoothie recipe. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Finish the recipe; draw the smoothie.

**Check learning:** Collect five recipes; check plurals and some / lots of.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Ta'mlar haqida yozish bolalarga yoqadi — 'mening sevimli sharbatim' bilan bog'lang.
> - Support: give a recipe template. Extension: add 'There aren't any …' for things they hate.
>

### Lesson 5: Reuse old things

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Story value (reuse old things), Talk time (making choices), Say it! ch |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson students can…) | • follow the story and say how to reuse old things<br>• make a choice: Would you like a mango or a pear? — A mango, please.<br>• pronounce /tʃ/ in chipmunk, fries, cheese, chair |
| **Key language** | • Would you like a (mango) or a (pear)? — A (mango), please.<br>• Sound: ch → /tʃ/ (chipmunks, fries) |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book story + audio<br>• Student's Book Say it! page<br>• real items (old jars, boxes) |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show an old jar: 'Is it trash? What can we do?' Elicit ideas (pencil pot, flower pot). | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Rubbish or treasure?** Show items; students say how to reuse them. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Story: predict from pictures, listen, answer 'What do they reuse? Why?' Discuss in Uzbek, then conclude in English: 'Reuse old things.' | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Talk time: model 'Would you like a mango or a pear?'; practice in pairs with fruit cards. Say it!: listen, repeat, find more ch words. | Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Fruit shop role-play**: pairs — shopkeeper offers two choices; customer chooses with 'please' and 'thank you'. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Chant the Say it! tongue twister. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Workbook story page; reuse one item at home and draw it.

**Check learning:** Listen for correct choices and polite language in the role-play.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'Would you like…?' — 'Xohlaysizmi?' Muloyim taklif; 'Do you want…?' dan ko'ra odobliroq.
> - /tʃ/ o'zbekcha 'ch' ga o'xshash — oson tovush; ammo 'chair' /tʃer/ — 'ch' + 'er' (qisqa 'e' va 'r') deb mashq qiling.
>

### Lesson 6: Plants we eat

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | CLIL Science — What parts of plants can we eat? |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson students can…) | • name the parts of a plant we eat: roots, stems, leaves, flowers, fruit, seeds<br>• say: We eat the roots of a carrot |
| **Key language** | • roots · stem · leaves · fruit · seeds<br>• We eat the (roots) of a (carrot). |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book CLIL pages<br>• real carrot, celery or cabbage, apple, rice<br>• a plant diagram |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show a carrot: 'Is this a fruit or a vegetable? Which part of the plant is it?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Which part?** Show real items; students point to the diagram part. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Teach the plant parts with the diagram. Sort items: carrot (root), celery (stem), cabbage (leaves), apple (fruit), rice (seeds). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Student's Book CLIL activities; students complete a two-column table: Plant \| Part we eat. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Plant plate**: students draw a plate with a meal using six parts of plants and label them. | S |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Students read their plate labels to a partner. Homework. | Ss-Ss |

**Homework:** Find three vegetables at home and say which part we eat.

**Check learning:** Sorting table (6 items) and plate rubric: labels (1), accuracy (1), neatness (1).

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Mahalliy misollar: sabzi (root), ko'k piyoz (leaves), olma (fruit), guruch (seed) — palov mavzusi bolalarga tanish.
> - Support: pictures for each part. Extension: write a full sentence for each part.
>
