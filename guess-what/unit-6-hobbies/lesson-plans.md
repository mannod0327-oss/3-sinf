# Unit 6 · Hobbies — Lesson plans

*8 lessons × 45 minutes · Guess What! · Grade 3*

### Unit at a glance

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Topic** | Hobbies and sports; what people do and when |

### Lesson 1: Hobby words

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — ten hobbies and sports |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name ten hobbies<br>• say what hobbies they like: I play the guitar. I do karate. |
| **Key language** | • play the piano / guitar / recorder, make models / films, do karate / gymnastics, play table tennis / badminton / volleyball |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards<br>• Student's Book Vocabulary page + audio<br>• Worksheet A exercises A–B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Greet the class. Ask 'What do you like doing after school?' and write **Hobbies** on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Charades**: pupils mime a hobby; the class guesses in Uzbek and you give the English phrase. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present the ten cards with actions. Show the verb patterns: **play** + instrument / ball game; **do** + karate / gymnastics; **make** + models / films. Student's Book: *Listen and point*, *Listen, point and repeat*, number game ('Is he playing the piano? Number 4!'). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label) and B (match with Uzbek). Play **What's missing?** | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Is he playing the piano?** Pairs play the Student's Book number game: one pupil asks yes / no questions until they guess the picture number. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says a phrase; pupils act it. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the phrases and draw your hobby (Worksheet A exercise F).

**Check learning:** Point at cards for 5 pupils; note gaps.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'play / do / make' — uchta fe'l, o'zbekchada ko'pincha 'qilmoq / chalmoq'. Jadval: play + gitara, do + karate, make + kino.
> - 'recorder' — bolalar fleytasi (blokflöyta). Mahalliy maktab musiqa xonasidagi 'nay' bilan solishtiring.
> - Support: pictures only. Extension: add two more hobbies.
>

### Lesson 2: She does karate on Sundays

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Grammar 1 — he / she + does / plays / makes; on Sundays, in the evening, after school |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say what a person does and when: She does karate on Sundays.<br>• use on + day, in the morning / afternoon / evening, after school |
| **Key language** | • She (does karate) (on Sundays). He plays / makes / does …<br>• on Saturdays · in the evening · after school |
| **Materials** | • A weekly hobby chart for one child (draw on the board)<br>• Flashcards<br>• Worksheet B exercise A |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What does your friend do on Saturdays?' pointing to a pupil and model 'She plays …'. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **What does she do?** Show a weekly hobby chart of an imaginary child and pupils guess the hobby from a mime. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Read the chart: 'Lily plays badminton on Saturdays. She does karate on Sundays. She makes models after school.' Underline the verb endings (plays, does, makes) and the time phrases. Point out the irregular forms do → does, go → goes. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Student's Book Grammar activities (listen, then true / false). Worksheet B exercise A (circle). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Hobby chart**: pupils draw a weekly chart for an imaginary friend and tell a partner: 'Bobur plays volleyball on Fridays.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Quick-fire: name a child and a day; pupils say the sentence. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Workbook grammar page; write three sentences about a friend's hobbies and when.

**Check learning:** Check -s / -es endings and the time phrases.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Vaqt predloglari: **on** + kun (on Sundays), **in** + kun qismi (in the evening), **after** school. Ularni uchta rangda yozing.
> - 'does' — bu yerda asosiy fe'l ('do karate'), 'do' ning he/she shakli.
> - Support: sentence frames. Extension: add 'and' to join two hobbies.
>

### Lesson 3: Does she do gymnastics?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Grammar 2 — Does she …? Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't. |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • ask and answer: Does she do gymnastics in the evening? Yes, she does.<br>• ask about time: When does he play volleyball? |
| **Key language** | • Does she (do gymnastics) (in the evening)? — Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't. |
| **Materials** | • Club timetables (Student's Book club notes or your own)<br>• Chant from the audio<br>• Worksheet B exercises B–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Does your friend play football?' and elicit 'Yes, he does.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Yes / no race**: two teams; you ask 'Does she play the piano?' pointing to a picture; fastest correct answer scores. | G |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Use the Student's Book club notes (two children and their clubs): 'Does he play tennis on Tuesdays? Yes, he does.' 'Does she play volleyball in the morning? No, she doesn't.' Build the pattern on the board. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Play the chant / Student's Book Grammar audio. Worksheet B exercises B (fill) and C (unscramble). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Club detectives**: pairs get different timetables; A asks yes / no questions to fill in their missing days. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher asks five questions about the timetables; pupils answer chorally. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Activity Book grammar page; write four questions about a friend's hobbies.

**Check learning:** Check Does + bare verb and short answers.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'Does' bor joyda asosiy fe'lga -s qo'shilmaydi: Does she **do**, Does he **play**.
> - Support: question frames on strips. Extension: ask 'When…?' questions.
>

### Lesson 4: What sports do you like?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Skills: Reading and writing — What sports do you like? |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • read a text about a boy and his sports and answer questions<br>• write about their favourite sport |
| **Key language** | • He goes to a football club on Tuesdays.<br>• favourite sport · healthy diet |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book Reading page<br>• Worksheet B exercises D–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Who likes football?' Count hands and write the class results. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Sports survey**: pupils stand and group themselves by favourite sport. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Before reading: look at the picture and predict. Read aloud once while pupils underline the sports. Check 'healthy diet' with pictures. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Silent reading, then the comprehension questions (Student's Book, then Worksheet B exercise D). Check in pairs. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | Writing: pupils write four sentences about their favourite sport using the help box (Worksheet B exercise E). | S |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Class chart: 'Football is our class favourite.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Finish the writing; add a drawing.

**Check learning:** Collect five texts; check verb endings and time phrases.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'goes to a club' — 'to'garakka qatnaydi'. 'go' → 'goes' (-es) ni alohida ko'rsating.
> - Support: cloze text. Extension: write about a sport they want to try.
>

### Lesson 5: Try new things

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Story value (try new things), Talk time (encouraging others), Say it! sh |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • follow the story and say why it is good to try new things<br>• encourage a friend: Come on, try it! You can do it! Well done!<br>• pronounce /ʃ/ in shark, fish, she, shop |
| **Key language** | • Come on — try it! · You can do it! · Well done!<br>• Sound: sh → /ʃ/ (sharks, fish) |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book story + audio<br>• Student's Book Say it! page |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What new thing did you try this year?' (Uzbek is fine). | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Try it!** Pupils try an unusual action (write with the other hand) while the class shouts 'You can do it!' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Story: predict from pictures, listen, answer 'Who can play? Who is afraid? Who helps?' Discuss the value and conclude in English: 'Try new things.' | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Talk time: model 'Come on — try it!' and practise in open and closed pairs with one pupil hesitating. Say it!: listen, repeat, find more sh words. | Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Try this!** Pairs role-play: one tries a new hobby (table tennis), the other encourages. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Chant the Say it! tongue twister. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Activity Book story page; try one new thing and tell the class.

**Check learning:** Listen for encouraging phrases and /ʃ/.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - /ʃ/ o'zbekcha 'sh' ga o'xshash — bu tovush bolalarga oson. 'fish' oxiridagi 'sh' ni cho'zib mashq qiling.
> - Ruhlantirish iboralarini har dars boshida ishlating: 'Well done!'
>

### Lesson 6: Musical instruments

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | CLIL Music — What type of musical instrument is it? |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name four instrument families: string, woodwind, brass, percussion<br>• say what type an instrument is: A guitar is a string instrument |
| **Key language** | • string · woodwind · brass · percussion · instrument<br>• A (piano) is a (string) instrument. |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book CLIL pages<br>• pictures of instruments (drum, dutar, flute, trumpet, guitar)<br>• recycled card, rice / beans, tape (to make a drum) |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Play a short clip or make a sound; ask 'What instrument is it?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Name that sound**: pupils guess the instrument from a sound or mime. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Introduce the four families with pictures. Include a local instrument: the dutar is a string instrument, the doira is a percussion instrument. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Student's Book CLIL activities; pupils sort instruments into four columns. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Make a drum**: from recycled card (Student's Book project); decorate and play a rhythm. | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Each group plays a rhythm; the class says 'It's a percussion instrument.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Find one instrument at home or in your neighbourhood and say its type.

**Check learning:** Sort instruments correctly (4 points) and explain one in English.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Mahalliy cholg'ular: dutar (string), doira (percussion), nay (woodwind), karnay (brass). Bu darsni o'zbek madaniyati bilan bog'lash tushunishni oshiradi.
> - Xavfsizlik: doira yasashda qaychi va yelimni nazorat qiling.
>

### Lesson 8: Review: Units 5 and 6

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Student's Book Review pages for Units 5 and 6 |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • recycle Unit 5 and Unit 6 language in games and puzzles<br>• show confidence with likes, does / doesn't and hobbies |
| **Key language** | • Unit 5 and Unit 6 language |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book Review (Units 5 and 6) + audio<br>• Activity Book review pages<br>• Flashcards from both units |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Greet the class and explain that today is a mixed review with word puzzles. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Two-unit quiz show**: two teams answer quick questions from both units. | G |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Go through the Review page: backwards-word puzzles and the matching tasks. Model one item. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Pupils do the Review activities in pairs (listening / word puzzles / speaking). Monitor and note errors. | Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | Pupils make their own backwards-word puzzle for a friend (like 'yalp llabyellov') and swap. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Pupils write one thing they can do well and one thing to practise. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Activity Book review pages.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Bu dars 3-chorak nazorat ishiga tayyorgarlik sifatida ham ishlaydi.
