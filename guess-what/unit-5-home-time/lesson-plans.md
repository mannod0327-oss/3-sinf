### Lesson 1: At home

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — ten things we do at home |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson students can…) | • name ten home activities<br>• say what they do at home: I watch TV. I do my homework. |
| **Key language** | • drink juice, eat a sandwich, do the dishes, play on the computer, read a book, watch TV, do homework, listen to music, make a cake, wash the car |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards<br>• Student's Book Vocabulary page + audio<br>• Worksheet A exercises A–B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Greet the class and ask 'What do you do after school?' (Uzbek is fine). Write **Home time** on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Mime the chore**: students mime an activity; the class shouts the Uzbek word; you give the English phrase. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present the ten phrases with actions and flashcards. Choral and individual repetition. Student's Book: *Listen and point*, *Listen, point and repeat*. Note 'do' with homework / dishes, 'make' with cake, 'watch' with TV. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label the pictures) and B (match with Uzbek). Play **What's missing?** | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Mime and guess** in groups: 'Are you washing the car?' — 'Yes, I am.' Keep the -ing forms simple and oral. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says a phrase, students do the action; then they say it back without a prompt. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the phrases and draw your favorite home activity (Worksheet A exercise F).

**Check learning:** Point at cards for 5 students; note gaps.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'do / make / watch' fe'llari: 'do homework', 'make a cake', 'watch TV'. O'zbekchada hammasi 'qilmoq' — shuning uchun fe'l + ot juftliklarini jadval qilib yozing.
> - 'homework' sanalmaydigan ot: 'a homework' deb aytilmaydi.
> - Support: flashcards with pictures only. Extension: add two home activities of their own.
>

### Lesson 2: He likes… He doesn't like…

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Grammar 1 — third person -s and doesn't + like / enjoy + -ing |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson students can…) | • say what he / she likes or doesn't like: He doesn't like reading books.<br>• use verb + -ing after like and enjoy: likes watching TV |
| **Key language** | • He (doesn't like) (reading books).<br>• She likes / enjoys (listening to music). |
| **Materials** | • Picture cards of family members (mom, dad, brother)<br>• Flashcards<br>• Worksheet B exercise A |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Does your brother like soccer?' (yes / no, in any language) and write 'likes' on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Like / don't like lines**: students move to one side of the room for 'I like…' and the other for 'I don't like…'. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show a boy (Karim) with thumbs up for watching TV and thumbs down for doing the dishes. Say: 'Karim likes watching TV. He doesn't like doing the dishes.' Write the rule: he / she + likes; doesn't + like. Verb + -ing after like / enjoy. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Student's Book Grammar activities. Worksheet B exercises A (circle) and B (complete with words from the box). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Family talk**: students tell a partner two sentences about a family member: 'My mom likes cooking. She doesn't like washing the car.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Quick-fire: show a picture and a thumb; students say the full sentence. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Workbook grammar page; write three sentences about your family's likes.

**Check learning:** Check likes / doesn't like and -ing forms in the family talk.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Uchinchi shaxs -s: he/she/it **likes**. O'zbek tilida bunday qo'shimcha yo'q, shuning uchun doskada doimo qizil rangda yozing.
> - 'doesn't' dan keyin fe'l asl holatda: He doesn't **like** (likes emas). Bu eng keng tarqalgan xato.
> - Support: sentence frames. Extension: add a reason with 'because' (oral).
>

### Lesson 3: Does he enjoy it?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Grammar 2 — Does he / she …? Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't. |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson students can…) | • ask and answer yes / no questions: Does he enjoy doing the dishes?<br>• use short answers: Yes, he does. / No, she doesn't. |
| **Key language** | • Does he (enjoy) (doing the dishes)? — Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.<br>• Does she like (making cakes)? |
| **Materials** | • Activity cards (a picture + a thumb up/down)<br>• Chant from the audio<br>• Worksheet B exercises C–D |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Does Anna like reading?' about a student, pointing to her. Elicit 'Yes, she does.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Yes / no cards**: hold up the 'does' and 'doesn't' cards as the class answers your questions. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Board table: Does he/she + verb …? → Yes, he/she does. / No, he/she doesn't. Show that after 'does' the verb is bare (enjoy, like). Drill with picture questions. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Play the chant / Student's Book Grammar audio. Worksheet B exercises C (unscramble) and D (read and answer). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Guess who it is**: a student chooses a famous person or a classmate; the class asks 'Does he like soccer?' (only yes/no answers) until they guess. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher asks five quick questions about a picture family; students answer in chorus. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Workbook grammar page; write four yes / no questions about a friend.

**Check learning:** Listen for Does + bare verb and the correct short answer.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Savol shakli: **Does** + he/she + fe'l (asl holat). 'Does he likes' — eng ko'p xato; har safar to'g'rilab boring.
> - Qisqa javob: Yes, he **does**. / No, he **doesn't**. Uzun javobni talab qilmang.
> - Support: give a question frame. Extension: students ask 'Why?' and answer 'Because…'
>

### Lesson 4: Are you helpful at home?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Skills: Listening — Are you helpful at home? + writing about a family |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson students can…) | • listen for what people do and do not like doing at home<br>• write four sentences about a family member |
| **Key language** | • helpful · at home<br>• He / She likes … He / She doesn't like … |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book Listening page + audio<br>• Worksheet B exercise E<br>• picture cards of chores |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Are you helpful at home?' and count the hands. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Chore race**: two teams mime chores; the first to guess the phrase scores a point. | G |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Pre-listening: look at the pictures, name chores. Play the audio twice: first for gist (who helps), then to check likes and dislikes. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Check in pairs and with the class. Students read the Saturday text (Worksheet B exercise D) and find who does what. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | Writing: using the help box, students write four sentences about a family member's likes and dislikes. Volunteers read aloud. | S |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Students swap texts and check a sentence they like. Homework. | Ss-Ss |

**Homework:** Finish the text; add a drawing of the family.

**Check learning:** Collect five texts: check -s, doesn't + verb, -ing forms.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Tinglash: rasmlarni avval nomlang — so'zlar eshitilganda tanish bo'ladi.
> - Support: provide a cloze text. Extension: write what they do to help at home.
>

### Lesson 5: Say sorry, forgive

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Story value (show forgiveness), Talk time (suggesting food to make), Say it! th |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson students can…) | • follow the story and explain in simple words why we forgive friends<br>• suggest what to make: Let's make a cake! — Good idea! / No, let's make …<br>• pronounce /θ/ in three, panther, thank you |
| **Key language** | • Let's make (a cake). — Good idea! / OK. / No, let's make (a salad).<br>• Sound: th → /θ/ (three, panthers, thank you) |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book story + audio<br>• Student's Book Say it! page<br>• pictures of food |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Do you say sorry when you're wrong?' Write **sorry / forgive** on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Say sorry**: pairs practice 'I'm sorry.' — 'That's OK.' with different situations. | Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Story: predict from the pictures, listen, answer 'Who is sorry? Who forgives?' Discuss in Uzbek, then conclude in English: 'Friends forgive.' | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Talk time: model 'Let's make a cake! — Good idea!', practice in open and closed pairs with food pictures. Say it!: put your tongue between your teeth; repeat three / thank you / panther. | Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Class menu**: groups decide what to make for a class party using Let's… / Good idea!, then present their menu. | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Chant the Say it! tongue twister. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Workbook story page; suggest a meal to cook at home.

**Check learning:** Listen for correct /θ/ and Let's … in the menu task.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'th' (/θ/) — til uchi tishlar orasida. O'zbek tilida bu tovush yo'q, ko'p bolalar 's' yoki 't' deyishadi ('sree', 'tree'). Ko'zgu bilan mashq qiling.
> - 'Let's' = 'Keling, …ylik'. 'Good idea!' — maqullash.
>

### Lesson 6: Where do people live?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | CLIL Geography — Where do people live? |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson students can…) | • name different places people live (city, village, mountains, desert)<br>• say: People live in (cities). Some people live in (tents). |
| **Key language** | • city · village · mountains · desert · tent<br>• People live in (villages). |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book CLIL pages<br>• pictures of homes<br>• A4 paper and pencils |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Do you live in a city or a village?' and count the answers. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Where is it?** Show pictures of homes; students say 'city / village / mountains / desert'. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Compare homes around the world (apartment, house, yurt / tent, mountain house). Introduce the sentence frames. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Student's Book CLIL activities. Students match people to places in a short table. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **My home poster**: students draw their home and its surroundings and write one sentence. | S |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Gallery walk: students read two classmates' posters. Homework. | Ss-Ss |

**Homework:** Ask an older relative where people lived in your area long ago.

**Check learning:** Poster rubric: picture (1), sentence (1), neatness (1).

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'yurt' — 'o'tov' (ko'chmanchi uyi) — o'zbek madaniyatiga bog'lang.
> - Mahalliy misollar: Samarkand (city), a village in Khorezm, Chimgan mountains, Kyzylkum desert.
>
