### Lesson 1: Places in school

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — ten school places |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name ten places in a school<br>• say where people usually are: You read in the library |
| **Key language** | • reception, dining hall, library, classroom, Science room, gym, Art room, Music room, playground, sports field<br>• This is the … / You (read) in the … |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards<br>• Student's Book Vocabulary page + audio<br>• Worksheet A (exercises A–B)<br>• Plan of your school drawn on the board |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Greet the class. Ask 'Where are we now?' — 'We're in the classroom.' Write **At school** on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Walk and talk**: take the class on a two-minute walk around the corridor or draw the school plan on the board and ask 'What's this place?' — pupils say it in Uzbek, you give the English word. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Stick the flashcards on the school plan. Teach each place with an action or sound (library = finger on lips, gym = lift arms, music room = sing). Choral and individual repetition. Student's Book: *Listen, point and repeat*. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | **What's missing?** and **Slow reveal** with the cards; then Worksheet A exercise A (label the pictures) and B (match the sentences to the places) in pairs. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Guess the place**: one pupil mimes an activity (reading, eating, painting), the class asks 'Are you in the library?' (yes / no) until they guess. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Race: two teams write as many school places as they can on the board in one minute. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the ten words and draw your dream school with five labelled places (Worksheet A, exercise F).

**Check learning:** Check which pupils can name all places without looking (board-race score).

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'dining hall' (Britaniya) va 'cafeteria' (AQSh) — bir xil joy. 'Science room' = 'science lab'.
> - O'zbek maktablarida 'oshxona' (ovqatlanish xonasi) — 'dining hall' ga mos keladi.
> - Support: pupils match picture to word first. Extension: add 'Where do you …?' questions.
>

### Lesson 2: Where are they?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Grammar 1 — Where are they? They're in / on the … |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • ask and answer: Where are they? They're on the sports field<br>• choose in or on correctly: in the library, on the playground |
| **Key language** | • Where are (they)? — They're (on the sports field).<br>• in the (library) · on the (playground) |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards on the school plan<br>• Student's Book Grammar page 1<br>• Worksheet B, exercise A |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Where is Aziz?' (pointing at an absent pupil's seat) — 'He's not here.' Introduce 'Where is / are…?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | Play **Hide the card**: hide a flashcard behind a pupil; class asks 'Is it in the library?' until they guess. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Put two or three name cards (Anna, Tom, Lily) on the plan. Say: 'Where are Tom and Lily? They're in the gym.' Show the pattern on the board with colours: **in** + closed room (classroom, library, gym), **on** + outdoor area (playground, sports field). Drill with picture cards. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Student's Book Grammar exercises. Worksheet B, exercise A — do items 1–2 together, rest alone, then check in pairs. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Where are they?** Pairs: pupil A holds a picture of children in a school place, pupil B asks 'Where are they?' and A answers 'They're in/on …'. Swap cards and repeat. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher points to a place on the plan; pupils chorus 'They're in the …'. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Activity Book grammar page; write three sentences: Where are your family now? (in / at home, at work).

**Check learning:** Listen for correct in / on in the pair work; note who uses them confidently.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - O'zbek tilida joylashuv qo'shimchalari (-da) bir xil; inglizchada esa **in / on / at** ajratiladi. Oddiy qoida: yopiq xona → in; ochiq maydon → on.
> - 'They're' → 'they are'. Kichik doskada 'they + are' deb yozib ko'rsating.
> - Support: colour-code in (blue) and on (green). Extension: add 'near / next to'.
>

### Lesson 3: What are you doing?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Grammar 2 — present continuous: What are you doing? We're playing baseball |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • ask and answer: What are you doing? I'm reading / We're playing<br>• form -ing verbs (reading, playing, running, painting) |
| **Key language** | • What are (you) doing? — (We)'re (playing baseball).<br>• I'm / He's / She's / We're / They're + verb-ing<br>• reading, singing, painting, running, playing |
| **Materials** | • Action cards (draw or use flashcards)<br>• Chant from the audio<br>• Worksheet B exercises B–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask five pupils 'What are you doing now?' — 'I'm sitting. I'm listening.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Simon says** with -ing: 'Simon says: you're jumping!' Pupils act and say 'I'm jumping.' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Mime reading and say 'I'm reading.' Write am/is/are + -ing on the board; show the five subject forms. Point out the spelling of running (double n) — practise on the board. Mime three actions and ask 'What am I doing?' | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Play the chant / Student's Book Grammar audio; do the action drill. Worksheet B, exercises B and C. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Mime and guess** in groups of four: one pupil mimes, others ask 'Are you playing football?' / 'What are you doing?' — use both question forms. | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Pupils stand; you say 'Everybody is singing!' — they sing and say 'We're singing.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write five sentences about what people in your family are doing now.

**Check learning:** Check am/is/are agreement: write three wrong sentences on the board and ask pupils to fix them.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - '-ing' shakli o'zbek tilidagi '-yapti / -moqda' ga o'xshaydi: 'o'qiyapman' = 'I'm reading'. Bu o'xshashlikdan foydalaning.
> - Ko'p xato: 'I reading' (am tushib qoladi). Har safar 'I'm' ni baland ovoz bilan ayting.
> - Support: give a sentence frame 'I'm ___ing'. Extension: negative 'I'm not running.'
>

### Lesson 4: Places in our school

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Skills: Reading and writing — What places can you find in your school? |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • read a short text about a school and answer questions<br>• write three sentences about their own school |
| **Key language** | • Our school has a … and a … · At break time we're …<br>• There is/are is not needed yet — use 'has' |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book Reading page<br>• Worksheet B, exercises E–F<br>• highlighters or coloured pencils |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What places are there in our school?' Brainstorm quickly on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Word snake**: write a long string of school words without spaces on the board; pupils separate them. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Before reading: look at the text's pictures and predict. Read aloud once while pupils underline the school places they know. Check meaning of any unknown words with pictures. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Pupils read again silently and complete the comprehension questions (Student's Book, then Worksheet B exercise E). Check in pairs, then whole class. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | Writing: pupils write three sentences about their school using the help box in Worksheet B exercise F. Volunteers read theirs aloud. | S |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Swap texts: each pupil ticks one good sentence in a friend's text and adds a smiley. Homework. | Ss-Ss |

**Homework:** Finish the writing; bring a photo or drawing of a place in your school.

**Check learning:** Collect five texts; look for school words, present continuous and capital letters.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Matn o'qishdan oldin rasmlarni ko'rib, taxmin qiling — bu tushunishni osonlashtiradi.
> - Yozuv: 'Our school has a library.' — 'has' (bitta narsa) va 'have' (ko'p) farqini ko'rsating.
> - Support: give a gapped model text. Extension: add two sentences about what people are doing.
>

### Lesson 5: Keep it clean

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Story value (keep your environment clean), Talk time (offering help), Say it! i / igh |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • follow the story and say why we should keep the school clean<br>• offer and accept help: Can I help you? — Yes, please. / No, thank you.<br>• pronounce /aɪ/ in tiger, night, light, like |
| **Key language** | • Can I help you? — Yes, please. / No, thank you.<br>• Sound: i / igh → /aɪ/ (tiger, night, light) |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book story + audio<br>• Student's Book Say it! page<br>• a bin and some scrap paper |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Drop a piece of paper on the floor and ask 'Is this OK?' Discuss in Uzbek, then 'Keep our school clean!' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Clean-up race**: two teams throw scrap paper into a bin, saying 'Clean, clean, clean!' | G |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Story: predict from pictures, listen and follow, answer 'Who helps? Who is not helping?' Discuss the value and conclude in English: 'We keep our school clean.' | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Talk time: model 'Can I help you?'; practise in open pairs, then closed pairs with real tasks (carry a bag, open a door). Say it!: listen, repeat, then brainstorm more i / igh words. | Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Helpers**: pairs invent a two-line dialogue where one offers help and the other accepts or refuses politely, then act it out. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Class chant of the Say it! tongue twister. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Activity Book story page; help someone at home and say 'Can I help you?'

**Check learning:** Listen for Can I help you? and polite answers in the pair work.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - /aɪ/ tovushi o'zbekcha 'ay' ga yaqin. 'night' ni 'nayt' deb mashq qiling, 'g' aytilmaydi.
> - Tozalik mavzusini maktab hayotiga bog'lang: 'Keling, sinfimizni toza saqlaymiz.'
>

### Lesson 6: Recycle it!

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | CLIL Science — What materials can we recycle? |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name four recyclable materials: paper, plastic, glass, metal<br>• say what can be recycled: We can recycle paper |
| **Key language** | • paper · plastic · glass · metal · recycle<br>• We can recycle (paper). / It's made of (glass). |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book CLIL pages<br>• real objects (a can, a plastic bottle, paper, a jar) or pictures<br>• four boxes or bags labelled with the materials |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Hold up an empty bottle: 'What is it? Is it rubbish?' Introduce 'recycle'. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Sort it!** Pupils put real objects into the four labelled boxes and say 'It's paper.' | G |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Explain the recycling symbol and the four materials with real objects. Write 'We can recycle paper, plastic, glass and metal.' on the board. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Student's Book CLIL activities (watch / match). Pupils complete a short sorting table in their notebooks. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Recycling poster** in groups: title 'Please recycle!', pictures of four materials, one sentence for each. Display around the classroom. | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Each group reads one sentence; class holds up the correct material object. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Bring one item that can be recycled (clean) for the class recycling box.

**Check learning:** Poster rubric: four materials (1), sentences (1), neat work (1).

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'recycle' — 'qayta ishlash'. O'zbekistondagi 'chiqindini saralash' tashabbuslariga bog'lang.
> - Xavfsizlik: faqat toza, o'tkir qirrasiz buyumlar olib kelinsin (shisha emas, plastik/qog'oz).
>

### Lesson 8: Review: Units 1 and 2
