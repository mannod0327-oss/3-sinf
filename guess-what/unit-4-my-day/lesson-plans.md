### Lesson 1: My day words

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — ten daily routines |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • name ten daily routines<br>• say what they do: I get up. I go to school. |
| **Key language** | • get up, get dressed, have breakfast, clean my teeth, go to school, have lunch, go home, have dinner, have a shower, go to bed |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards<br>• Student's Book Vocabulary page + audio<br>• Worksheet A exercises A and C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Greet the class. Ask 'What time do you get up?' (answers in Uzbek are fine) and write **My day** on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Mime the morning**: pupils stand and mime waking up, getting dressed, brushing teeth — you say the phrase and the class repeats and acts. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Present the ten cards in daily order with actions. Choral and individual repetition. Student's Book: *Listen and point*, *Listen, point and repeat*. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercise A (label pictures) and C (put the day in order). Check with the cards on the board. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Mime and guess** in pairs: one pupil mimes a routine, the other says 'You get up!' Swap. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says a routine; pupils do the action without speaking. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the ten phrases. Draw your morning and label it (Worksheet A, exercise F).

**Check learning:** Point to cards at random for 5 pupils; note those who need review.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'have breakfast / lunch / dinner' — 'have' + ovqat nomi. O'zbekchada 'nonushta qilmoq', lekin inglizchada 'eat' emas 'have' ishlatiladi.
> - 'clean my teeth' (Britaniya) = 'brush my teeth' (AQSh) — ikkalasi ham to'g'ri.
> - Support: daily-order picture strip on the wall. Extension: add 'wash my face'.
>

### Lesson 2: What time is it?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Telling the time (o'clock, half past) and the pattern I get up at seven o'clock |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • tell the time on the hour and the half hour<br>• say: I get up at seven o'clock. I have dinner at half past seven. |
| **Key language** | • What time is it? — It's (seven o'clock). / It's (half past seven).<br>• I (get up) at (seven o'clock). |
| **Materials** | • A big cardboard clock with moving hands<br>• Worksheet A exercise B, Worksheet B exercise B<br>• Clock faces (drawn by pupils) |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What time is it now?' Show the real classroom clock. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Clock mime**: use your arms as clock hands: 'It's three o'clock!' Pupils copy. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Teach o'clock first (minute hand on 12), then half past (minute hand on 6). Show each with the cardboard clock and say it three times. Then link to routines: 'I get up at seven o'clock.' | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercise B (match clocks and times) and Worksheet B exercise B (write the times). Student's Book time activities. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Set the clock**: pairs — A says 'half past eight', B sets their cardboard clock; then swap and add a routine: 'I go to school at half past eight.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Race: teacher says a time, pupils hold up the right clock drawn on mini-whiteboards. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Draw four clocks and write the times. Write two sentences: I … at …

**Check learning:** Check who confuses o'clock and half past.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - O'zbekcha 'soat yetti yarim' = 7:30 = inglizcha 'half past seven'. So'zma-so'z o'xshash, shuning uchun pupillar tez o'zlashtiradi.
> - 'at' + vaqt: at seven o'clock. O'zbek tilidagi '-da' qo'shimchasiga mos keladi.
> - Support: clock with numbers written. Extension: add 'a quarter past' (oral).
>

### Lesson 3: What time do you get up?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Grammar — What time do you …? I … at …; So do I. / I don't. |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • ask and answer: What time do you get up? I get up at seven.<br>• agree or disagree: So do I. / I don't. |
| **Key language** | • What time do you (get up)? — I (get up) at (seven o'clock).<br>• So do I. / I don't. |
| **Materials** | • Routine flashcards + clocks<br>• Student's Book Grammar pages<br>• Worksheet B exercises A, C, D |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask three pupils 'What time do you get up?' and write the answers on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Find someone who…**: pupils stand and look for classmates who get up at the same time. | Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Model the dialogue with a pupil: 'I get up at seven.' — 'So do I.' — 'I get up at half past six.' — 'I don't. I get up at seven.' Write it on the board and mark So do I = the same / I don't = different. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Student's Book Grammar activities (listen and choose, then speak). Worksheet B exercises A (circle), C (fill) and D (unscramble). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Class survey**: pupils ask three classmates two questions and respond with So do I / I don't, then report: 'Aziz and I get up at seven.' | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Whole-class chain: 'I get up at … ' — next pupil 'So do I.' / 'I don't. I …'. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Activity Book grammar page; ask a family member two questions and write the answers.

**Check learning:** Check do / don't, at + time and So do I in the survey.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'So do I' = 'Men ham' (xuddi shunday); 'I don't' = 'Men esa yo'q'. O'zbek tilidagi 'men ham' bilan bog'lang.
> - Ko'p xato: 'What time you get up?' (do tushib qoladi). Doskada savol sxemasini: What time + do + you.
> - Support: sentence strips. Extension: add a reason: 'I get up at six because I go to school early.'
>

### Lesson 4: A healthy lifestyle

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Skills: Reading and writing — Do you have a healthy lifestyle? |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • read a short text about a day and answer questions<br>• write four sentences about their own day |
| **Key language** | • healthy · exercise · sleep · breakfast<br>• I get up at …, I go to bed at … |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book Reading page<br>• Worksheet B exercises E–F |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Are you healthy? What do you do to be healthy?' (answers in Uzbek and English). | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Healthy or not?** Show picture cards (fruit, sweets, sport, computer); pupils show thumbs up or down. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Pre-reading: predict from the pictures and title. Read aloud once; pupils underline the times they hear. Check words: healthy, sleep, exercise. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Silent reading and questions (Student's Book, then Worksheet B exercise E). Check in pairs. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | Writing: using the frame in Worksheet B exercise F, pupils write four sentences about their day. Volunteers read aloud. | S |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Pupils swap texts and tick 'healthy' things; class discusses what makes a healthy day. Homework. | Ss-Ss |

**Homework:** Finish the writing; add one healthy idea ('I play football.').

**Check learning:** Collect five texts; check at + time, routine phrases and capital letters.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'healthy' — sog'lom; 'lifestyle' — hayot tarzi. Oddiy so'zlar bilan: 'sport, sleep, good food'.
> - Support: provide a gapped text. Extension: compare two friends' days.
>

### Lesson 5: Take exercise!

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Story value (take exercise), Talk time (asking the time), Say it! ue / ew / oo |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • follow the story and say why exercise is good<br>• ask the time politely: Excuse me, what time is it? It's half past three.<br>• pronounce /uː/ in blue, chew, food, moon |
| **Key language** | • Excuse me, what time is it? — It's (half past three). — Thank you.<br>• Sound: ue / ew / oo → /uː/ (blue, chew, food) |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book story + audio<br>• Student's Book Say it! page<br>• cardboard clocks |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Do two minutes of exercise with the class (stretch, jump) and ask 'Do you like exercise?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Exercise clock**: call a time; pupils do that many jumps (seven o'clock = seven jumps). | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Story: predict from the pictures, listen, answer 'Who takes exercise? Why?' Discuss in Uzbek, then conclude in English: 'Take exercise. It's good for you.' | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Talk time: model 'Excuse me, what time is it?'; practise in pairs using the cardboard clocks. Say it!: listen, repeat, then find more oo / ue / ew words. | Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **What time is it, Mr Wolf?** The class asks the time; the 'wolf' answers with a time until they say 'It's dinner time!' — then everyone runs. | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Chant the Say it! tongue twister. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Activity Book story page; do exercise with a family member.

**Check learning:** Listen for Excuse me and a correct time answer in the pairs.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'Excuse me' — 'Kechirasiz'. Mulozamat iborasini har doim birga o'rgating.
> - /uː/ — o'zbekcha 'u' dan uzun. 'food' va 'foot' farqini juftliklar bilan mashq qiling.
>

### Lesson 6: Time around the world

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | CLIL Maths — What's the time around the world? |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • compare times in different places using a simple time-zone map<br>• say: It's twelve o'clock in Tashkent. It's ten o'clock in Moscow. |
| **Key language** | • It's (twelve o'clock) in (Tashkent). It's (ten o'clock) in (Moscow).<br>• earlier · later |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book CLIL pages<br>• a world map or globe<br>• paper plates to make clocks |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show a globe. Ask 'Where is Uzbekistan?' and find Tashkent. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Noon somewhere**: 'It's twelve o'clock in Tashkent. What time is it in Moscow?' — pupils guess. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Explain that different places have different times. Use cities with no summer-time change: Moscow is two hours earlier, Dubai one hour earlier, Delhi half an hour later and Tokyo four hours later than Tashkent. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Student's Book CLIL activities (time-zone map and questions). Pupils complete a small table: City \| Time when it is 12:00 in Tashkent. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Time-zone clocks** in groups: make four paper-plate clocks and set them to the times of four cities, label them and present. | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Each group says one sentence: 'It's four o'clock in Tokyo.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Ask a family member if they have friends or relatives in another country; find the time there.

**Check learning:** Check the group clocks are correct (Moscow 10, Dubai 11, Delhi 12:30, Tokyo 16 when it is 12 in Tashkent).

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Bu yerda yozgi vaqtga o'tmaydigan shaharlarni tanlang (Moskva, Dubay, Dehli, Tokio) — javoblar yil bo'yi to'g'ri bo'ladi. Toshkent = UTC+5.
> - Support: provide ready-made clock faces. Extension: pupils calculate the time in a fifth city.
>
