### Lesson 1: The days of the week

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — Monday to Sunday |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say the seven days in order<br>• say which day is today, tomorrow and yesterday |
| **Key language** | • Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday<br>• Today is … / Tomorrow is … / Yesterday was … |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards (one day each, with the memory picture)<br>• Student's Book Vocabulary page + audio<br>• Worksheet A exercises A–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Greet the class and ask 'What day is it today?' — write the weekday on the board with today's date. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Human calendar**: seven pupils hold the day cards and stand in a line in the wrong order; the class tells them where to go ('Monday, here!'). | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Teach the days with the flashcards and a rhythm (clap on the stressed syllable: MON-day, TUES-day). Use the pictures as memory aids (moon, fire, wind, lightning, heart, planet, sun). Student's Book: *Listen and point*, *Listen, point and repeat*. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (order), B (match with Uzbek) and C (complete the words). Chant the days forwards and backwards. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Today, tomorrow, yesterday**: in pairs, pupil A says 'Today is Wednesday.' Pupil B says 'Tomorrow is Thursday.' Then 'Yesterday was Tuesday.' Swap. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Whole-class chant of the week with the actions. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn to spell the seven days. Draw this week's calendar with one picture per day (Worksheet A, exercise F).

**Check learning:** Ask six pupils 'What day is it tomorrow?' without help.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Kun nomlari bosh harf bilan yoziladi: Monday, Tuesday… (o'zbek tilida kichik harf).
> - 'Wednesday' talaffuzi /ˈwenzdeɪ/ — 'd' aytilmaydi. 'Thursday' — 'th' tovushiga e'tibor bering.
> - Support: use the picture cards while ordering. Extension: name the month and date too.
>

### Lesson 2: Have we got Science on Tuesday?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Grammar 1 — Have we got …? Yes, we have. / No, we haven't. |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • ask and answer about the timetable: Have we got Maths on Monday?<br>• use on + day of the week and short answers Yes, we have. / No, we haven't. |
| **Key language** | • Have we got (Science) on (Tuesday)? — Yes, we have. / No, we haven't.<br>• Maths, English, Science, Art, Music, PE |
| **Materials** | • Class timetable (real or drawn on the board)<br>• Subject flashcards<br>• Worksheet B exercises A–B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Review days with the human-calendar game. Ask 'What lessons have we got today?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Guess the lesson**: mime a subject (painting, singing, running) — the class says the subject. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Project the class timetable. Ask and answer with the whole class: 'Have we got Maths on Monday? Yes, we have.' 'Have we got PE on Wednesday? No, we haven't.' Underline **on + day** and the short answers. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Pairs: use the Student's Book timetable to ask and answer. Worksheet B exercises A (circle) and B (read Anna's timetable and answer). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Timetable interview**: pupils ask three classmates 'Have you got … on …?' and record yes / no on a grid, then report: 'We've got Art on Friday.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Whole class: teacher says a subject and day — pupils answer Yes, we have / No, we haven't. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Copy your own school timetable in English (days and subjects).

**Check learning:** Listen for have/haven't and on + day; note errors for the next lesson.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - O'zbekcha 'bizda … bor / yo'q' — inglizcha 'we have got / we haven't got'. 'Got' ni tashlab ketishmasin.
> - Kun oldidagi predlog: **on Monday**, lekin 'in the morning'. Ikkalasini ajratib yozib qo'ying.
> - Support: colour-code the days. Extension: ask about two subjects in one question.
>

### Lesson 3: What club has she got?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Grammar 2 — has got, clubs and parts of the day |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • ask and answer: What club has she got in the evening? She's got swimming club.<br>• use in the morning / afternoon / evening |
| **Key language** | • What (club) has (she) got (in the evening)? — She's got (swimming club) (in the evening).<br>• in the morning · in the afternoon · in the evening |
| **Materials** | • Club posters (swimming, chess, art, football)<br>• Student's Book Grammar page 2<br>• Worksheet B exercises C–D |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What clubs have we got at school?' and list them on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Who is it?** Describe a pupil's clubs ('He's got chess club on Monday.') — the class guesses the pupil. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show a weekly club timetable for a fictional child (Lily). Model the question and answer, then build the he / she forms: 'has got' = 's got. Compare: We've got / She's got. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Student's Book Grammar activities. Worksheet B exercises C (unscramble) and D (fill in). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Class club chart**: groups collect and report 'Amir has got chess club on Tuesday afternoon.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Quick-fire: teacher names a child and a day; the class says the club. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Activity Book grammar page; write two sentences about a friend's clubs.

**Check learning:** Check has / have and 's got in the group reports.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'has got' — he/she/it; 'have got' — I/you/we/they. O'zbek tilida bunday farq yo'q, shuning uchun jadval yordam beradi.
> - Qisqa shakl: **She's got** = She has got. **We've got** = We have got.
>

### Lesson 4: My favourite day

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Skills: Listening — Have you got a favourite day of the week? + writing a timetable |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • listen for days and subjects and complete a timetable<br>• write a short text about their school day |
| **Key language** | • My favourite day is …, because I've got … on … <br>• days, subjects |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book Listening page + audio<br>• Worksheet B, exercise E<br>• a blank timetable grid |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Have you got a favourite day?' and count hands for each day on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Timetable bingo**: pupils write five day-subject pairs; you call pairs, they cross them out. | S |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Pre-listening: read the instructions and look at the picture. Play the audio twice — first for gist (who likes which day), then to complete the timetable. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Check answers in pairs, then with the class. Pupils read Anna's timetable (Worksheet B, exercise B) again and find the busiest day. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | Writing: pupils write four sentences about their favourite school day using the help box. Volunteers read aloud. | S |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Graph the favourite days on the board and say 'Friday is our class favourite.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Finish the text; draw your timetable for one day.

**Check learning:** Collect five texts: check on + day, subjects and 've got.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Tinglash: ikki marta qo'ying; birinchi marta kunlarni, ikkinchi marta fanlarni toping.
> - Support: provide a half-filled timetable. Extension: add 'because it's …' (oral).
>

### Lesson 5: Be resourceful

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Story value (be resourceful), Talk time (asking if places are open), Say it! oa / ow |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • follow the story and explain in simple words what 'resourceful' means<br>• ask if a place is open: Is the library open today? Yes, it is. / No, it's closed.<br>• pronounce /əʊ/ in goat, snow, boat, road |
| **Key language** | • Is the (library) open (on Sundays)? — Yes, it is. / No, it's closed.<br>• Sound: oa / ow → /əʊ/ (goats, snow, boat) |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book story + audio<br>• Student's Book Say it! page<br>• open/closed signs for school places |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show an OPEN and a CLOSED sign and ask 'Is our library open today?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Open or closed?** Hold up a sign and a place flashcard; pupils say 'The library is open!' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Story: predict from pictures, listen, answer 'What problem have they got? How do they solve it?' Discuss 'resourceful' (finding a clever way to solve a problem) in Uzbek and English. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Talk time: model the question and answers, practise in open and closed pairs with the signs. Say it!: listen, repeat, brainstorm more oa / ow words. | Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Open for business**: pairs create a timetable for three places (library, shop, gym) and role-play asking if they are open. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Chant the Say it! tongue twister slowly then fast. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Activity Book story page; ask at home when a local shop is open.

**Check learning:** Listen for Is … open? and correct short answers.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'open' = ochiq, 'closed' = yopiq. Vaqt so'rash uchun 'on Sundays' (yakshanba kunlari).
> - /əʊ/ diftongi — 'o' + 'u' ga yaqin; 'snow' ni 'snou' deb mashq qiling.
>

### Lesson 6: Night and day animals

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | CLIL Science — Which animals are nocturnal? |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say what nocturnal and diurnal mean in simple words<br>• sort animals into day and night animals |
| **Key language** | • nocturnal · at night · during the day<br>• An owl is nocturnal. It sleeps during the day. |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book CLIL pages + video if available<br>• animal pictures (owl, bat, rabbit, tortoise…)<br>• a two-column table: Day \| Night |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Turn the lights off for a moment: 'Is it day or night?' Introduce 'night'. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Day or night?** Show animal pictures; pupils stand (day) or sit (night) for each. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Explain 'nocturnal' with the owl and bat; compare with animals that are active during the day. Build the table on the board. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Student's Book CLIL activities; pupils complete their own Day \| Night table. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Fact cards** in groups: each group writes two sentences about one nocturnal animal and draws it. | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Groups read their cards; the class says 'day animal' or 'night animal'. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Find one nocturnal animal from Uzbekistan and draw it.

**Check learning:** Fact card rubric: correct sentence (1), drawing (1), teamwork (1).

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'nocturnal' — 'tungi'. Mahalliy misol: 'yarqanot' (bat), 'boyo'g'li' (owl).
> - Fan darsi bilan integratsiya: hayvonlarning kun tartibi.
>
