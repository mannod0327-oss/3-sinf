# Welcome — Lesson plans

*4 lessons × 45 minutes · Guess What! · Grade 3*

_The Welcome unit has four lessons in the annual plan (Story, Talk time, Say it! and CLIL share the last lesson)._

### Unit at a glance

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Topic** | The course friends; questions; months of the year and birthdays |
| **Vocabulary** | Lucas, Max, Lily, Tom, Anna; the twelve months |
| **Grammar** | • Revision of questions (What's your name? How old are you? Where are you from?)<br>• When's your birthday? It's in (December). |
| **Skills** | Reading: Have you got an email penpal? |
| **Say it! (phonics)** | a / ai — snakes, tails |
| **Story value** | Work together |
| **Talk time** | Asking for permission |
| **CLIL** | Art — What can you see in a landscape painting? |

### Lesson 1: Meet the friends

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | The five course characters; revision of personal questions |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • introduce themselves: My name's … I'm … years old. I'm from …<br>• ask and answer: What's your name? How old are you? Where are you from? |
| **Key language** | • What's your name? — My name's … · How old are you? — I'm … · Where are you from? — I'm from …<br>• Lucas, Max, Lily, Tom, Anna |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book Welcome pages + audio<br>• name labels<br>• character pictures (Lucas, Max, Lily, Tom, Anna) |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Greet the class warmly: 'Welcome to Grade 3!' Give each pupil a name label. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Name ball**: throw a soft ball; the catcher says 'My name's …' then throws to someone else. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Introduce the five friends with the Student's Book pictures: 'This is Lucas. He's nine. He's from …' Pupils listen and point to the characters. Teach the three questions with gestures. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Pairs: ask and answer the three questions. Worksheet B exercises A–B. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Class interview**: pupils walk around and fill a card: name, age, town for three classmates. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Report: 'Bobur is nine. He's from Tashkent.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write three sentences about yourself.

**Check learning:** Listen to each pupil introduce themselves; note those who need support.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Yangi o'quv yilida ismlar va yoshni so'rash — eng yaxshi muloqot boshlanishi. Hammani gapirishga undang.
> - 'I'm from Tashkent' — 'Men Toshkentdanman'. 'from' (-dan) ni albatta qo'shish kerak.
> - Support: give answer frames. Extension: add 'I've got a brother.'
>

### Lesson 2: Months of the year

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Vocabulary — the twelve months |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say the twelve months in order<br>• say which month it is now and when they were born |
| **Key language** | • January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards (one month each)<br>• Student's Book Welcome pages + audio<br>• Worksheet A exercises A–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What day is it? What month is it?' Write the month on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Human year**: twelve pupils hold the month cards and stand in a circle in the wrong places; the class tells them where to go. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Teach the months with the flashcards in groups of four (winter / spring / summer / autumn). Use the pictures as memory aids. Chant them with a rhythm. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (order), B (match with Uzbek) and C (complete the words). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Line up by birthday**: pupils ask 'When's your birthday?' and line up from January to December without speaking Uzbek. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Whole-class chant of the months with actions. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn to spell the months. Draw your birthday cake (Worksheet A exercise F).

**Check learning:** Ask five pupils 'What month is after …?'

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Oylar bosh harf bilan yoziladi: March (o'zbek tilida 'mart' kichik harf).
> - Qiyin so'zlar: February (Feb-ru-ary), August, September. Bo'g'inlarga bo'lib mashq qiling.
> - Support: use the picture cards. Extension: name the season for each month.
>

### Lesson 3: When's your birthday?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Grammar — When's your birthday? It's in (December). + Reading: an email penpal |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • ask and answer: When's your birthday? It's in December.<br>• read a short penpal email and write one |
| **Key language** | • When's your birthday? — It's in (December).<br>• penpal · email · Dear … / Hi … |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book Welcome pages + audio<br>• Worksheet B exercises C–E<br>• birthday cards or paper |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Sing or chant 'Happy birthday'. Ask 'Whose birthday is in January?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Birthday circle**: pupils stand in a circle; you call a month and those born in it swap places. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Model the question and answer with two pupils, then write it on the board with **in** + month highlighted. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Pairs: class survey of birthdays. Worksheet B exercises C (unscramble) and D (read the penpal email). | Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | Writing: pupils write an email to a penpal (Worksheet B exercise E) and swap with a partner. | S |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Class graph of birthdays by month; 'Most birthdays are in …'. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Finish your email; ask two family members when their birthdays are.

**Check learning:** Collect five emails: check My name is / I'm / It's in and capital letters.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'in' + oy (in March), 'on' + sana (on the 5th). Bu sinfda faqat 'in' ni o'rgating.
> - Elektron xat (email): Hi/Dear … va oxirida ism. Yozuv odatini shakllantiring.
> - Support: give a gapped email. Extension: add 'I like …' sentences.
>

### Lesson 4: Work together

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Story value (work together), Talk time (asking for permission), Say it! a / ai, CLIL Art (landscape) |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • follow the story and say why it is good to work together<br>• ask permission politely: Can I open the window, please? — Yes, you can. / Sorry, you can't.<br>• pronounce /eɪ/ in snake, tail, rain, cake<br>• name parts of a landscape: mountains, river, trees, sky |
| **Key language** | • Can I (open the window), please? — Yes, you can. / Sorry, you can't.<br>• Sound: a / ai → /eɪ/ (snakes, tails)<br>• landscape · mountains · river · trees · sky |
| **Materials** | • Student's Book story + audio + Say it! page<br>• Student's Book CLIL page<br>• paper and crayons |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask two pupils to carry a big box together: 'Can you do it alone? Let's work together!' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Group puzzle**: each group builds a simple jigsaw; the fastest group says what helped them. | G |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Story: predict from pictures, listen, answer 'Who works together? What happens?' Talk time: model the permission dialogue with two pupils; practise in pairs. Say it!: listen and repeat /eɪ/. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Art (CLIL): look at the landscape painting in the Student's Book and name what you can see: 'I can see mountains.' Pupils complete a simple table: Foreground \| Background. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Group landscape**: each group draws one large landscape, each pupil adds one thing and asks permission ('Can I use the green pencil, please?'). | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Groups present their painting: 'We can see a river and some trees.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Activity Book Welcome pages; draw a landscape you know in Uzbekistan.

**Check learning:** Listen for permission phrases and please / thank you.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'Can I …?' — ruxsat so'rash. 'May I' ham to'g'ri, ammo bu sinfda 'Can I' yetarli.
> - /eɪ/ diftongi o'zbek tilida yo'q — 'ey' ga yaqin; 'snake' ni 'sneyk' deb mashq qiling.
> - Bu dars mazmuni 2 ta darslik bo'limini (Story + CLIL) birlashtirgan; vaqt yetmasa Art qismini keyingi darsga qoldiring.
>
