## Unit at a glance

- **In the book:** Round-Up 3, Unit 13 (prepositions of place) and Unit 14 (imperative)
- **Vocabulary:** in, on, under, next to, behind, in front of, between (+ commands: sit down, stand up, open your book, be quiet, listen, don't run, don't shout)
- **Grammar:** Where is the ball? It's in / on / under / next to / behind / in front of / between …
- **Grammar:** Imperatives: Sit down. · Open your book. · Don't run. · Let's play.

## Lessons

1. Where is the ball? — *Prepositions of place: in, on, under, next to, behind, in front of, between*
2. Do! Don't! — *Imperatives: Sit down. Don't run.*
3. Treasure hunt — *Prepositions and imperatives together*
