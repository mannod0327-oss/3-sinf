### Lesson 1: Where is the ball?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Prepositions of place: in, on, under, next to, behind, in front of, between |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say where something is<br>• ask and answer: Where is the ball? It's under the table. |
| **Key language** | • in · on · under · next to · behind · in front of · between<br>• Where is …? It's … |
| **Materials** | • A ball and a box (real objects)<br>• Flashcards of the seven pictures<br>• Grammar card |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Hide a small toy in the classroom and ask 'Where is it?' Let pupils guess with Uzbek, then model 'It's under the book.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Hot and cold**: one pupil leaves; the class hides an object; on return the pupil asks 'Is it in the bag?' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Use the ball and the box to show all seven prepositions, then show the flashcards. Drill with gestures (hands in / on / under). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label the pictures), B (circle) and D (complete). Pairs place objects and describe. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Barrier game**: pairs sit back to back; A places a ball on a picture of a room and describes it; B draws. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher places the ball; pupils chorus 'It's in front of the box!' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Draw your room and write five sentences: 'My bag is next to the chair.'

**Check learning:** Listen for correct prepositions in the barrier game.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'in / on / under' o'zbekchada -da, -ning ustida, -ning tagida; rasm + harakat bilan o'rgating.
> - 'in front of' — uch so'zli ibora: bir birlik sifatida o'rgating.
> - Support: use real objects. Extension: add 'opposite' and 'near'.
>

### Lesson 2: Do! Don't!

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Imperatives: Sit down. Don't run. |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • understand and give classroom commands<br>• say what we do and don't do in class |
| **Key language** | • Sit down. · Stand up. · Open your book. · Be quiet. · Don't run. · Don't shout. |
| **Materials** | • Command flashcards<br>• Worksheet B exercises A–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Give commands in English with gestures: 'Stand up. Sit down.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Simon says**: pupils follow only the commands with 'Simon says'. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show the command cards and act each one. Write the pattern: verb (Do) / Don't + verb. Stress: no 'you', no -s. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises A (circle), B (fill), C (unscramble) and D (class rules text). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Class rules poster**: groups write four rules with Do and Don't and draw icons for each. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Groups present their poster; class acts out the rules. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write four home rules: 'Don't …', 'Wash your hands.'

**Check learning:** Check verb base forms (Sit, not Sits) and Don't + verb.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Buyruq = fe'lning asl shakli: 'Sit down' (Sits emas). O'zbekchada ham buyruq oddiy: 'O'tir!'.
> - Muloyimlik: 'Sit down, please.' — 'please' ni doim ayting.
> - Support: picture cards with commands. Extension: add 'Let's …'.
>

### Lesson 3: Treasure hunt

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Prepositions and imperatives together |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • give and follow instructions to find something<br>• use prepositions and imperatives in one text |
| **Key language** | • Look under the chair. · Go to the window. · It's behind the door. |
| **Materials** | • A hidden 'treasure' (toy or sweet)<br>• Clue cards<br>• Worksheet A exercise E; Worksheet B exercise E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show the treasure box: 'Where is the treasure? Let's find it!' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Give a command**: pupils give the class one-word commands in turn (stand, jump, sit). | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Read the first clue aloud: 'Look under the big book.' Model following it and reading the next clue. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Groups follow four clue cards with imperatives and prepositions; each clue leads to the next. | G |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Write your own clues**: pairs write three clues for another pair using 'Look …' and prepositions. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | The group that finds the treasure reads the last clue aloud. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write two treasure-hunt clues for your family.

**Check learning:** Check that clues use correct prepositions and imperatives.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Xavfsizlik: yugurmang, sekin yuring — 'Don't run!' qoidasini amalda qo'llang.
> - Support: give a clue frame. Extension: pupils draw a map.
>
