### Lesson 1: my, your, his, her, our, their

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Possessive adjectives and possessive 's |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say who things belong to: This is my pen. That is Aziz's bag.<br>• use 's for one owner |
| **Key language** | • my · your · his · her · its · our · their<br>• Aziz's book · Malika's bag |
| **Materials** | • Real objects (pens, books, bags)<br>• Flashcards<br>• Worksheet A exercises A, B; Worksheet B exercise A |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Hold up a pupil's bag: 'Whose bag is this?' Elicit 'It's Bobur's bag.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Lost property**: put five objects on a table; pupils claim theirs: 'It's my pen!' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write the pronoun row (I, you, he …) and the matching possessive (my, your …). Show 's with real owners (Aziz's book). Colour the boy words blue and the girl words red. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label) and B (match), Worksheet B exercise A (circle). Check in pairs. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Pass and say**: pupils pass objects around a circle and say 'This is Anna's ruler. Her ruler is red.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher points at objects; pupils say 'That is … 's … ' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write six sentences with my, your, his, her about things in your bag.

**Check learning:** Note who confuses his and her.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'his / her': o'zbekchada bitta 'uning' — rasm bilan mashq qiling.
> - 'its' (uning — narsa/hayvon) bilan 'it's' (it is) farqi — hozircha eshitish darajasida.
> - Support: use real owners. Extension: add 'because' reasons.
>

### Lesson 2: mine, yours, his, hers

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Possessive pronouns: This pen is mine. |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • use mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs without a noun<br>• answer Whose … is this? |
| **Key language** | • This pen is mine. · That bag is hers. · Whose is this? — It's yours. |
| **Materials** | • Real objects<br>• Worksheet A exercise B; Worksheet B exercises B–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Whose is this?' holding different pupils' items. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Mine!** Pupils race to grab the item you name and say 'It's mine!' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write two sentences side by side: 'This is my pen.' / 'This pen is mine.' Show the pronoun table (mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs). Stress: no noun after mine / yours … | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercise B (match adjectives to pronouns) and Worksheet B exercises B and C. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Whose is it?** In groups, pupils put items in a bag; one pupil draws an item and asks 'Whose is this?' — the owner says 'It's mine!' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Quick-fire: point at things and pupils say 'It's his / hers / theirs.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Workbook: fill in the possessive pronoun table and write four sentences.

**Check learning:** Check that pupils do not add a noun after mine / hers.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'mine' = 'meniki' (-niki qo'shimchasi): 'Bu qalam meniki' = 'This pencil is mine'. O'xshashlik yordam beradi.
> - 'This is mine pen' — keng tarqalgan xato: mine dan keyin ot kelmaydi.
> - Support: pronoun table on the board. Extension: add Whose are these? They're Malika's.
>

### Lesson 3: this, that, these, those

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Near and far; one and many |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • use this / these for near things and that / those for far things<br>• ask What's this? What are those? |
| **Key language** | • This is a pen. · These are pens. · That is a chair. · Those are chairs. |
| **Materials** | • Objects near and far<br>• Worksheet B exercise B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Hold a book near you and point to a far one: 'This book … that book.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Near or far?** Point and say 'this' or 'that'; pupils raise hand near / arm far. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Stand at the front; show this / these with objects in your hands and that / those with objects at the back. Write the four forms in a 2 × 2 table (near/far, one/many). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercise B (circle). Pairs point at classroom objects and ask What's this? / What are those? | Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Classroom shop**: one pupil is the shopkeeper; customers ask for 'this pen' or 'those pencils' and point. | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Whole-class drill with pointing. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Draw four pictures (near / far, one / many) and label them this, these, that, those.

**Check learning:** Observe correct choice in the shop role-play.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Masofa tushunchasini **ishora** bilan bog'lang: yaqin — qo'l ichida, uzoq — qo'lni cho'zing.
> - 'these / those' ko'plik; 'these' /ðiːz/, 'those' /ðəʊz/ — 'th' talaffuziga e'tibor bering.
> - Support: gestures. Extension: add colours (that red pen).
>
