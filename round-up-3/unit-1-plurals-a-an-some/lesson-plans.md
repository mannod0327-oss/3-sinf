# Unit 1 · Plurals, a / an, some — Lesson plans

*3 lessons × 45 minutes · Round-Up 3 · Grade 3*

_Adapted for Grade 3 (age 8–9, CEFR A1): the ideas come from the Round-Up 3 unit; all exercises and texts are new._

### Unit at a glance

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **In the book** | Round-Up 3, Unit 1 — Plurals of countable and uncountable nouns |
| **Topic** | One or many? Regular and irregular plurals; a, an, some |
| **Vocabulary** | cat, box, bus, baby, leaf, child, man, foot, tooth, sheep, fish, mouse (+ milk, water, bread, rice) |
| **Grammar** | • Regular plurals: -s, -es, -ies (cats, boxes, babies) and -ves (leaves)<br>• Irregular plurals: children, men, feet, teeth, mice, sheep, fish<br>• a / an with one thing, some with many things and with milk, water, bread, rice |

### Lesson 1: Regular plurals

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | One or many: -s, -es, -ies, -ves |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say and write regular plurals (cats, boxes, babies, leaves)<br>• choose the correct ending |
| **Key language** | • one cat – two cats · one box – two boxes · one baby – two babies · one leaf – two leaves |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards (games/flashcards.pdf)<br>• Grammar card (grammar-card.pdf)<br>• Worksheet A exercises A, C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Greet the class and count objects in the room together: 'One desk, two desks …' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **How many?** Show one flashcard and then two: 'One cat. Two … ?' — pupils complete the plural. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Put four columns on the board (+s, +es, -ies, -ves) and sort the flashcards into them while saying the words. Underline the endings and point out the spelling change baby → babies. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (complete the plurals with pictures) and C (circle the correct spelling). Check with the class using whiteboards. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Plural race**: two teams write as many plurals as they can in two minutes from the flashcard words. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says a singular word; pupils clap and answer with the plural. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Workbook: write the plurals of ten nouns from your bag or room.

**Check learning:** Check the spelling of -es and -ies plurals on the whiteboards.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Ko'plik qo'shimchasini ajratib ko'rsating: -lar (o'zbekcha) = -s / -es. Raqamdan keyin ham ko'plik kerak: 'three box' emas, 'three boxes'.
> - '-es' ni aniq talaffuz qiling /ɪz/: boxes, buses.
> - Support: sorting with cards only. Extension: add leaf → leaves, knife → knives.
>

### Lesson 2: Irregular plurals

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | child → children, man → men, foot → feet, tooth → teeth, mouse → mice, sheep, fish |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say the seven irregular plurals<br>• use them in short sentences |
| **Key language** | • one child – two children · one man – two men · one foot – two feet · one tooth – two teeth · one mouse – two mice · one sheep – two sheep |
| **Materials** | • Picture cards of the irregular nouns<br>• Worksheet A exercises B, D, E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'How many children are there in our class?' — 'There are … children.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Odd words**: write ten plurals on the board; pupils stand when they hear one that is irregular. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show pairs of pictures (one / two) and build the list on the board in two colours: regular and irregular. Drill the irregular ones with gestures (feet = stamp, teeth = smile). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises B (match), D (word search) and E (true or false). Play **Plural dominoes** in pairs. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Picture dictation**: pupils listen and draw 'two sheep, three children, four mice' and compare. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Whole-class chant: 'child-children, man-men, foot-feet, tooth-teeth.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Learn the seven irregular plurals and draw them (Worksheet A exercise F).

**Check learning:** Check who says 'childs' or 'mans'; note for reteaching.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'childs', 'mans', 'foots' — bu eng keng tarqalgan xato (o'zbekcha -lar qoidasini umumlashtirish). Bu so'zlarni 'maxsus ro'yxat' sifatida yodlatish kerak.
> - 'sheep' va 'fish' — bir xil: 'one sheep, two sheep'.
> - Support: use the picture cards. Extension: add woman – women, person – people.
>

### Lesson 3: a, an or some?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | a / an with one thing; some with many and with milk, water, bread, rice |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • use a, an and some correctly<br>• say what they have got: I've got an apple and some bread |
| **Key language** | • a cat · an apple · some cats · some milk<br>• I've got … / We've got … |
| **Materials** | • Real or picture items (apple, egg, bread, milk)<br>• Worksheet B exercises A–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Show items one by one: 'What have I got? An apple. Some bread.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Sort the basket**: pupils put items into two boxes — *a / an (one)* and *some (many / uncountable)*. | G |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Explain: a before a consonant sound, an before a vowel sound; some for many or for things we don't count. Write five examples on the board. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises A (circle), B (fill), C (unscramble). Check in pairs. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Shopping basket**: pairs choose five items from a picture list and say 'I've got a book, two pencils and some paper.' | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Reading (Worksheet B exercise D) and writing (exercise E). Homework. | S |

**Homework:** Write what is in your school bag with a / an / some.

**Check learning:** Mark Worksheet B exercise E for a / an / some and plural endings.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - O'zbek tilida artikl yo'q — **a / an** ni tushirib qoldirish keng tarqalgan xato ('I have cat' o'rniga 'I have a cat').
> - 'an' — unli **tovush** oldidan: an apple, an orange, an elephant (harf emas, tovush).
> - Support: a / an sound sort with cards. Extension: add an hour, a university (oral).
>
