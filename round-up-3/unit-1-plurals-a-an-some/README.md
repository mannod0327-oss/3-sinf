## Unit at a glance

- **In the book:** Round-Up 3, Unit 1 — Plurals of countable and uncountable nouns
- **Vocabulary:** cat, box, bus, baby, leaf, child, man, foot, tooth, sheep, fish, mouse (+ milk, water, bread, rice)
- **Grammar:** Regular plurals: -s, -es, -ies (cats, boxes, babies) and -ves (leaves)
- **Grammar:** Irregular plurals: children, men, feet, teeth, mice, sheep, fish
- **Grammar:** a / an with one thing, some with many things and with milk, water, bread, rice

## Lessons

1. Regular plurals — *One or many: -s, -es, -ies, -ves*
2. Irregular plurals — *child → children, man → men, foot → feet, tooth → teeth, mouse → mice, sheep, fish*
3. a, an or some? — *a / an with one thing; some with many and with milk, water, bread, rice*
