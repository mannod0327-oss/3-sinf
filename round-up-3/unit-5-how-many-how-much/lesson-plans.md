|  |  |
|---|---|

### Lesson 1: some and any

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Countable and uncountable nouns; some (+), any (− ?) |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say if a noun can be counted<br>• use some in positive and any in negative sentences and questions |
| **Key language** | • There are some eggs. · There is some milk.<br>• There aren't any apples. · Is there any bread? |
| **Materials** | • Food picture cards (egg, apple, milk, bread)<br>• a picture of a fridge with some items<br>• Grammar card |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Open a picture of a fridge: 'What is there in the fridge?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Count it!** Show cards; pupils say 'We can count it: one egg, two eggs' or 'We can't: milk'. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Sort the cards into countable / uncountable. Write the positive / negative / question pattern and underline some and any. Use the fridge picture for examples. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label), D (complete the words) and E (word search); Worksheet B exercise A (circle some / any). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Spot the fridge**: pairs have two fridge pictures and ask 'Is there any milk?' to find the differences. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Quick-fire: show a card; pupils make a full sentence with some or any. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write five sentences about your kitchen with some and any.

**Check learning:** Listen for some / any in the fridge game.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - O'zbek tilida 'sanaladigan / sanalmaydigan' farq bor (non, sut). Xuddi shuni inglizchaga bog'lang.
> - 'some' ijobiy, 'any' inkor va so'roq. Istisno: taklif ('Would you like some tea?') — hozircha o'rgatmang.
> - Support: colour-code countable (blue) and uncountable (green).
>

### Lesson 2: How many? How much?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | How many + plural, How much + uncountable; many / much |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • ask and answer: How many eggs are there? How much milk is there?<br>• choose many or much |
| **Key language** | • How many (eggs) are there? — There are six. · How much (milk) is there? — A lot. |
| **Materials** | • Real or picture items in bags<br>• Worksheet A exercises B, C; Worksheet B exercise B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'How many pupils are there in our class?' and 'How much water is in this bottle?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Bag questions**: pupils ask 'How many apples are in the bag?' and guess; you show the answer. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write the two question patterns side by side with examples. Stress: How many + plural noun, How much + uncountable noun. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises B (match questions and answers) and C (circle many or much), Worksheet B exercise B. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Kitchen survey**: pupils ask 'How many eggs have you got at home?' 'How much bread?' and report. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Class chant: 'How many apples? How much milk?' with gestures. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Workbook: write six questions with How many / How much and answer them.

**Check learning:** Check pupils do not say 'How much eggs'.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'How much eggs' — keng tarqalgan xato, chunki o'zbekchada 'qancha' bitta. Ikkita so'zni (many / much) kartalarda ranglang.
> - Pul uchun ham 'How much is it?' — keyingi bo'limlarda takrorlanadi.
> - Support: sentence frames. Extension: add 'a lot of'.
>

### Lesson 3: a lot of, a few, a little

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Quantities: a lot of, a few, a little — at the bazaar |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • use a lot of, a few and a little correctly<br>• role-play buying food at the bazaar |
| **Key language** | • There is a lot of bread. · I have a few apples. · I want a little honey. |
| **Materials** | • Play money and food picture cards<br>• Worksheet B exercises C–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Put several cards on the table: 'There are a lot of apples. There are a few bananas.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Lots or few?** Show a card with many or few items; pupils say 'a lot of' or 'a few'. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show the small-quantity words: a few (countable), a little (uncountable). Compare with a lot of. Write three examples. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises C (unscramble), D (reading at the bazaar) and E (writing). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **At the bazaar**: shopkeepers and customers: 'Can I have a few apples and a little honey, please?' — 'Here you are.' | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Report: 'I bought …' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write a short shopping dialogue.

**Check learning:** Observe correct a few / a little use in the role-play.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'a few' (bir nechta — sanaladigan), 'a little' (ozgina — sanalmaydigan). Juftlik qilib yodlating.
> - Bozor kontekstidan foydalaning: 'Chorsu bozori' — bolalar uchun tanish vaziyat.
> - Support: model dialogue on the board. Extension: add prices (How much is it? — 5,000 sum).
>
