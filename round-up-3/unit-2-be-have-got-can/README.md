## Unit at a glance

- **In the book:** Round-Up 3, Unit 2 — Personal pronouns / 'be' / 'have got' / 'can'
- **Vocabulary:** swim, run, jump, ride a bike, sing, dance, draw, play football, climb, fly; pronouns I, you, he, she, it, we, they
- **Grammar:** be: I am · he / she / it is · you / we / they are (+ negative and questions)
- **Grammar:** have got: I / you / we / they have got · he / she / it has got
- **Grammar:** can / can't + verb: She can swim. He can't fly.

## Lessons

1. I am, you are, he is — *be with pronouns; negatives and questions*
2. I've got, she's got — *have got / has got (statements, negatives, questions)*
3. I can, I can't — *can / can't for ability; questions and short answers*
