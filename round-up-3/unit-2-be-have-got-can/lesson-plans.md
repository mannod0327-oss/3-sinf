# Unit 2 · be, have got, can — Lesson plans

*3 lessons × 45 minutes · Round-Up 3 · Grade 3*

_Adapted for Grade 3 (age 8–9, CEFR A1): the ideas come from the Round-Up 3 unit; all exercises and texts are new._

### Unit at a glance

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **In the book** | Round-Up 3, Unit 2 — Personal pronouns / 'be' / 'have got' / 'can' |
| **Topic** | Who I am, what I have and what I can do |
| **Vocabulary** | swim, run, jump, ride a bike, sing, dance, draw, play football, climb, fly; pronouns I, you, he, she, it, we, they |
| **Grammar** | • be: I am · he / she / it is · you / we / they are (+ negative and questions)<br>• have got: I / you / we / they have got · he / she / it has got<br>• can / can't + verb: She can swim. He can't fly. |

### Lesson 1: I am, you are, he is

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | be with pronouns; negatives and questions |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • use am / is / are with I, you, he, she, it, we, they<br>• ask and answer: Are you ten? Yes, I am. |
| **Key language** | • I am · you are · he / she / it is · we / you / they are<br>• I'm not · isn't · aren't · Are you …? |
| **Materials** | • Pronoun cards (I, you, he, she, it, we, they)<br>• Grammar card<br>• Worksheet A exercise A; Worksheet B exercise B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Greet the class and introduce yourself: 'I am Miss … I am a teacher.' Invite pupils to do the same. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Pronoun pick**: point to people and say the pronoun (you, he, she, we, they) — pupils repeat. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write the table on the board: I am / you are / he is … Colour the forms. Use the contractions I'm, you're, he's. Then show the negative (I'm not / he isn't) and questions (Are you …?). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercise A (circle) and Worksheet B exercise B (fill in). Pairs check each other. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Mingle**: pupils ask 'Are you nine?' 'Are you from Tashkent?' and answer with short answers. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Quick-fire: point at a pupil and say a pronoun; pupils say the full sentence 'He is …'. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write five sentences about yourself with am / is / are.

**Check learning:** Listen for am / is / are in the mingle activity.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'am / is / are' ni tushirmaslik: o'zbekcha 'Men ikkinchi sinfman' da bog'lovchi fe'l yo'q — inglizchada esa shart.
> - he / she — o'zbekcha 'u' ikkalasi uchun bitta: rasm bilan farqlang.
> - Support: show a pronoun–verb matching chart. Extension: add a question with Wh- words (oral).
>

### Lesson 2: I've got, she's got

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | have got / has got (statements, negatives, questions) |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say what they and others have got<br>• ask and answer: Has she got a cat? No, she hasn't. |
| **Key language** | • I've got · he's got · they've got<br>• haven't got · hasn't got · Have you got …? Has she got …? |
| **Materials** | • Picture cards (cat, dog, bike, computer, car)<br>• Worksheet A exercise B; Worksheet B exercise D |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Have you got a pet?' and collect answers on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Bag reveal**: take items from a bag: 'I've got a book. I've got an apple. Have you got an apple?' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show a boy and a girl with pictures. Model 'He has got a bike. She has got two cats.' Write the pattern with have for I / you / we / they and has for he / she / it. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercise B (fill), Worksheet B exercise D (reading). Pairs ask and answer with picture cards. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Find someone who has got…**: pupils walk around and find classmates with a pet, a bike, a brother. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Report back: 'Bobur has got a bike.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write what you and your family have got (six sentences).

**Check learning:** Check have / has agreement in the report-back.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'have got' = 'menda bor'. 'He have got' — keng tarqalgan xato; he/she/it → **has**.
> - Qisqa shakl: I've got, he's got (has got) — 'he's' = he is ham bo'lishi mumkin; kontekst bilan tushuntiring.
> - Support: use sentence frames. Extension: add number words (two brothers).
>

### Lesson 3: I can, I can't

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | can / can't for ability; questions and short answers |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say what they and animals can and can't do<br>• ask and answer: Can you swim? Yes, I can. |
| **Key language** | • I can swim. · He can't fly.<br>• Can you ride a bike? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't. |
| **Materials** | • Ability flashcards (games/flashcards.pdf)<br>• Worksheet A exercises C–E; Worksheet B exercises A, C, E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Can you swim?' — show thumbs up / down; count the class. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Animal charades**: mime an animal; the class guesses 'It can fly. It's a bird.' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Use the ability flashcards: 'I can swim. Can you swim?' Build the pattern on the board, including can't and the question form. Stress: can + bare verb. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises C (match animals and abilities), D (word search) and E (pictures). Worksheet B exercise A (circle). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Find someone who can…**: pupils ask 'Can you dance? Can you ride a bike?' and record names on a chart. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Class result: 'Nine pupils can swim.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write three things you can do and three you can't do (Worksheet B exercise E).

**Check learning:** Listen for can + bare verb in the survey.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'can' dan keyin fe'l o'zgarmaydi: She can **swim** (swims emas).
> - 'can't' talaffuzi /kɑːnt/ — 'can' /kæn/ bilan adashtirmang; urg'u va ovoz cho'zilishiga e'tibor.
> - Support: pictures for each verb. Extension: add adverbs (well, fast).
>
