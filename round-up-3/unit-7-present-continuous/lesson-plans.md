### Lesson 1: What is she doing?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Present continuous — statements and -ing spelling |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say what people are doing now: She is reading<br>• spell -ing correctly (make → making, run → running) |
| **Key language** | • I am reading · he is singing · they are dancing<br>• running · swimming · making |
| **Materials** | • Action flashcards<br>• Grammar card<br>• Worksheet A exercises A–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'What are you doing now?' — 'I'm sitting. I'm listening.' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Freeze mime**: pupils mime an action; you freeze them and ask the class 'What is he doing?' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Mime and say 'I am reading.' Write am / is / are + -ing on the board and colour the parts. Show three spelling rules with examples (play, make, run). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (circle), B (label the pictures) and C (write the -ing form). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Picture talk**: pairs describe what people in a picture are doing: 'The girl is dancing. The boys are running.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Quick-fire: teacher mimes; pupils say the full sentence. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write six sentences about what people in your family are doing now.

**Check learning:** Check am / is / are and the -ing spelling.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - O'zbekcha '-yapti, -moqda' (o'qiyapti) = inglizcha **am/is/are + -ing**. O'xshashlikdan foydalaning.
> - Eng ko'p xato: 'She reading' (is tushib qolgan). Har gapda bog'lovchi fe'lni baland ayting.
> - Support: -ing cards. Extension: add negative sentences.
>

### Lesson 2: Is he running? No, he isn't.

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Present continuous — negatives, questions and short answers |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say what is not happening: He isn't running<br>• ask and answer: Are they dancing? Yes, they are. |
| **Key language** | • He isn't running. · I'm not sleeping.<br>• Are they dancing? — Yes, they are. / No, they aren't. |
| **Materials** | • Action flashcards<br>• Worksheet B exercises A–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Are you sleeping?' — 'No, I'm not.' Elicit short answers. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Yes / no mime**: one pupil mimes; the class asks 'Are you swimming?' until they guess. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write the negative (am not, isn't, aren't) and the question pattern (Am I, Is he, Are they) on the board. Show short answers. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises A (circle), B (fill in) and C (unscramble). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Yes / no 20 questions**: one pupil chooses a picture; the class asks 'Is she running?' (yes / no) to find it. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says a wrong sentence about a picture; pupils correct it: 'No, she isn't reading. She's writing.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write three negative sentences and three questions about a picture.

**Check learning:** Listen for inversion in questions (Is she …? not She is …?).

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Savol: 'Is she reading?' (Is oldinda). O'zbek tilida ohang yoki '-mi' — inglizchada so'z tartibi o'zgaradi.
> - Qisqa javob: Yes, she **is**. (is'ni qisqartirmang: 'Yes, she's' xato).
> - Support: question frames. Extension: add What / Where questions (oral).
>

### Lesson 3: Now or every day?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Present continuous (now) vs present simple (every day) |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • choose present continuous for now and present simple for habits<br>• contrast: I read every day. I'm reading now. |
| **Key language** | • I play football every Sunday. I'm playing now.<br>• now · at the moment · every day · usually |
| **Materials** | • Picture pairs (habit and now)<br>• Worksheet B exercises D–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Write 'every day' and 'now' on the board and ask 'What do you do every day? What are you doing now?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Now or every day?** Read sentences; pupils stand for 'now' and sit for 'every day'. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show two columns on the board: every day → present simple, now → present continuous. Sort key words and sample sentences. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises D (reading) and E (writing). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Mime it**: one pupil mimes an action and the class says 'He is swimming now. He swims every Friday.' | S |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher says key words; pupils hold up 'simple' or 'continuous' cards. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write two sentences for four verbs: one for every day and one for now.

**Check learning:** Mark Worksheet B exercise E for choosing the right tense.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - Signal so'zlar yordam beradi: **now, at the moment, Look!** → -ing. **every day, usually, always** → -s.
> - Ikkalasini bir jadvalda ranglab ko'rsating: ko'k — odat, qizil — hozir.
> - Support: signal-word table. Extension: add a third column (yesterday — later).
>
