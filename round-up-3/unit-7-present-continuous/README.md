## Unit at a glance

- **In the book:** Round-Up 3, Unit 8 — Present continuous
- **Vocabulary:** run, swim, read, write, sing, dance, cook, draw, play football, sleep (+ now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!)
- **Grammar:** am / is / are + verb-ing (running, making, swimming)
- **Grammar:** negative: I'm not · he isn't · they aren't; questions: Is she …? Are they …?
- **Grammar:** now (present continuous) vs every day (present simple)

## Lessons

1. What is she doing? — *Present continuous — statements and -ing spelling*
2. Is he running? No, he isn't. — *Present continuous — negatives, questions and short answers*
3. Now or every day? — *Present continuous (now) vs present simple (every day)*
