## Unit at a glance

- **In the book:** Round-Up 3, Unit 7 — Present simple
- **Vocabulary:** get up, eat, drink, read, play, watch, study, walk, sleep, cook (+ always, usually, sometimes, never)
- **Grammar:** I / you / we / they + verb; he / she / it + verb-s (-es, -ies)
- **Grammar:** don't / doesn't + verb; Do / Does + subject + verb?
- **Grammar:** adverbs of frequency: always, usually, sometimes, never

## Lessons

1. He plays, she watches — *Present simple — affirmative; the third-person -s / -es / -ies*
2. I don't, he doesn't, do you? — *Present simple — negatives and questions*
3. How often? — *Adverbs of frequency: always, usually, sometimes, never*
