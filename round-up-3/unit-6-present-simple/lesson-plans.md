### Lesson 1: He plays, she watches

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Present simple — affirmative; the third-person -s / -es / -ies |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say what people do every day using I / we / they and he / she<br>• spell -s, -es and -ies endings |
| **Key language** | • I play · she plays · he watches · she goes · he studies |
| **Materials** | • Verb flashcards<br>• Grammar card<br>• Worksheet A exercises A–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask three pupils 'What do you do after school?' and write the answers on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Mime and guess**: mime a verb; pupils say 'He plays!' (third person). | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write two columns: I / we / they (no change) and he / she / it (+ s). Sort the verbs; highlight -es (watches, goes, does) and -ies (studies). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (circle), B (label) and C (fill in with the verb in brackets). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Family facts**: pupils write and say three sentences about a family member: 'My dad drinks tea. He reads a book.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Quick-fire: teacher says a pronoun + verb; pupils say the full sentence. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write six sentences about what people in your family do every day.

**Check learning:** Check for missing -s in the family facts.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - '-s' qo'shimchasi o'zbek tilida yo'q — **eng ko'p xato**: 'He play'. Doim 'he / she / it + s' qoidasini baland ovoz bilan takrorlang.
> - -es / -ies: 'watches' /ɪz/ ni alohida mashq qiling.
> - Support: give the -s ending on cards. Extension: add adverbs (always, usually).
>

### Lesson 2: I don't, he doesn't, do you?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Present simple — negatives and questions |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say what they and others do not do<br>• ask and answer Do you …? Does she …? |
| **Key language** | • I don't drink coffee. · She doesn't watch TV.<br>• Do you play football? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't. |
| **Materials** | • Picture cards<br>• Worksheet B exercises A–C |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Do you watch TV in the morning?' and elicit short answers. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Truth or lie?** Pupils say sentences about themselves; the class guesses: 'Do you play the piano?' | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Write the negative and question patterns on the board (don't / doesn't + verb; Do / Does + subject + verb). Underline: after doesn't / does, the verb has **no -s**. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises A (circle), B (fill in) and C (unscramble). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Find someone who…**: pupils ask 'Do you drink milk?' and write names; then report: 'Bobur doesn't drink milk.' | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher asks five yes / no questions about a picture; pupils answer. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write five negative sentences about yourself and five questions for a friend.

**Check learning:** Listen for do / does and the bare verb after them.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'Does he plays?' — xato; 'does' dan keyin asl fe'l: 'Does he **play**?'.
> - Qisqa javoblar: Yes, he **does**. / No, he **doesn't**. — ko'p takrorlang.
> - Support: question frames. Extension: add Wh- words (What do you …?).
>

### Lesson 3: How often?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Adverbs of frequency: always, usually, sometimes, never |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • say how often they do things<br>• interview a partner about daily habits |
| **Key language** | • I always get up at seven. · She usually walks to school. · He never watches TV in the morning. |
| **Materials** | • A frequency line on the board (never — sometimes — usually — always)<br>• Worksheet B exercises D–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Ask 'Do you always eat breakfast?' and show the frequency line. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Stand on the line**: call a sentence ('I eat breakfast'); pupils stand at always / usually / sometimes / never. | G |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show the four adverbs with percentages on the line (100% / 80% / 50% / 0%). Write two sentences: adverb before the verb. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises D (reading) and E (writing with adverbs). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Habit interview**: pairs ask 'Do you always …? How often do you …?' and write three sentences about their partner. | Ss-Ss |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Report: 'Malika never eats cheese.' Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write four sentences with always, usually, sometimes and never about your week.

**Check learning:** Check word order (adverb before the verb).

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'always / usually / sometimes / never' gapda **fe'l oldida**: 'I **always** eat'. O'zbekchada odatda fe'l oldida ('men doim yeyman') — bu yerda o'xshashlik bor.
> - Support: frequency line visible. Extension: add 'every day / twice a week'.
>
