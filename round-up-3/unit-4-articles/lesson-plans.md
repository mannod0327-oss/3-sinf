# Unit 4 · Articles: a, an, the — Lesson plans

*3 lessons × 45 minutes · Round-Up 3 · Grade 3*

_Adapted for Grade 3 (age 8–9, CEFR A1): the ideas come from the Round-Up 3 unit; all exercises and texts are new._

### Unit at a glance

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **In the book** | Round-Up 3, Unit 4 — Articles |
| **Topic** | Which little word? a / an / the / nothing |
| **Vocabulary** | apple, orange, egg, elephant, umbrella, ice cream, ant, banana, dog, book (+ the sun, the moon) |
| **Grammar** | • a before a consonant sound (a dog), an before a vowel sound (an egg)<br>• the for a thing we both know, or only one (the sun)<br>• no article with plural things in general (I like dogs) and with names |

### Lesson 1: a or an?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | a / an with one thing |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • use a before consonant sounds and an before vowel sounds<br>• say: an apple, a banana |
| **Key language** | • a dog · a banana · a book · an apple · an egg · an elephant · an umbrella |
| **Materials** | • Flashcards (games/flashcards.pdf)<br>• Two boxes labelled a and an<br>• Grammar card |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Hold up a flashcard: 'What's this? It's an apple.' Write 'an apple' on the board. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **a or an?** Pupils stand for 'an' and sit for 'a' as you say the word. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Sort the flashcards into two boxes (a / an). Underline the first sound and say it: /æ/, /e/, /ɪ/, /ɒ/, /ʌ/ → vowel sounds → an. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercises A (label with articles), C (circle) and D (word search). Pairs sort the flashcards. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Shopping list**: pupils write five things they want to buy with the correct article and read it out. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Quick-fire: teacher says a word, pupils say 'a …' or 'an …'. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write ten nouns with a / an (five from your room, five from your bag).

**Check learning:** Listen for a / an in the shopping list activity.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - O'zbek tilida artikl yo'q — **'I have cat'** xatosi juda keng tarqalgan. Har bir yakka ot oldida a / an tekshiring.
> - Qoida harfga emas, **tovushga** bog'liq: an hour /aʊə/, a university /juː/.
> - Support: sorting cards only. Extension: add adjectives (an old umbrella).
>

### Lesson 2: the

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | the: something we know, the second time, or only one |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • use the for something already mentioned or unique<br>• tell a mini-story: I have a dog. The dog is brown. |
| **Key language** | • I have a dog. The dog is brown.<br>• the sun · the moon · the sea · the teacher |
| **Materials** | • Picture cards<br>• Worksheet A exercise E; Worksheet B exercises A, B |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Say 'I have a pet. The pet is a cat.' and ask pupils 'What's my pet?' | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Story chain**: pupil A says 'I have a cat.' Pupil B says 'The cat is black.' and adds 'I have a …' for C. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Show the rule with two sentences: first mention = a / an, second mention = the. Add things that are unique (the sun, the moon). | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet A exercise E (write the) and Worksheet B exercises A and B. | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Two-sentence stories**: pairs write and tell a short story with 'a' first and 'the' second. | Ss-Ss / G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Volunteers read their story; class checks a / the. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Write a four-sentence story about an animal using a and the.

**Check learning:** Check a → the in the stories.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'the' tanish narsa: suhbatdosh nimani nazarda tutganimni biladi. O'zbekchada buni 'o'sha' bilan ifodalash mumkin.
> - 'the' talaffuzi: undosh oldida /ðə/, unli oldida /ði/ — hozircha faqat /ðə/.
> - Support: give sentence starters. Extension: add adjectives.
>

### Lesson 3: a, an, the or nothing?

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Choose the right article (or none) |
| **Aims**  (by the end of the lesson pupils can…) | • choose a, an, the or no article in short texts<br>• use no article with plurals in general and names |
| **Key language** | • I like dogs. · Aziz is my friend. · We have lunch at one. |
| **Materials** | • Article cards (a, an, the, —)<br>• Worksheet B exercises C–E |
| **Time** | 45 minutes |

| Min | Stage | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Greeting & organisation** | Write 'I like ___ ice cream. / I have ___ apple.' on the board and ask which word fits. | T-Ss |
| 5 | **Warm-up** | **Article cards**: pupils hold up a / an / the / — cards for gaps you read aloud. | T-Ss |
| 10 | **Presentation** | Sum up on the board with four examples (one per column of the grammar card). Explain that plural general nouns and names take no article. | T-Ss |
| 12 | **Controlled practice** | Worksheet B exercises C (unscramble), D (reading) and E (writing). | S / Ss-Ss |
| 10 | **Freer practice / production** | **Article auction**: teams 'buy' correct sentences with play money; wrong sentences lose money. | G |
| 5 | **Wrap-up & homework** | Teacher reads three sentences; pupils hold up the right article card. Homework. | T-Ss |

**Homework:** Correct five sentences with article mistakes (on your worksheet).

**Check learning:** Mark Worksheet B exercise E for articles.

> **Teacher tips (o'qituvchi uchun)**
>
> - 'I like the dogs' (hamma itlarni emas, umuman) — xato; umumiy fikrda artikl ishlatilmaydi.
> - 'We have lunch at one' — ovqat nomlari oldida artikl yo'q.
> - Support: articles in a table. Extension: write a riddle using a / the.
>
