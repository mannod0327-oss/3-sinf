## Unit at a glance

- **In the book:** Round-Up 3, Unit 4 — Articles
- **Vocabulary:** apple, orange, egg, elephant, umbrella, ice cream, ant, banana, dog, book (+ the sun, the moon)
- **Grammar:** a before a consonant sound (a dog), an before a vowel sound (an egg)
- **Grammar:** the for a thing we both know, or only one (the sun)
- **Grammar:** no article with plural things in general (I like dogs) and with names

## Lessons

1. a or an? — *a / an with one thing*
2. the — *the: something we know, the second time, or only one*
3. a, an, the or nothing? — *Choose the right article (or none)*
